## Purpose

This catalog lists reusable headline patterns for landing page hero sections, organized by type, with examples across Lead Gen, SaaS, and Ecommerce verticals.

Headlines make or break the page. 80% of visitors decide to stay or leave based on the hero section alone, and the headline is the first thing they read.

## Headline fundamentals

### The 5-second rule

A visitor must understand the core value proposition within 5 seconds of landing on the page. The headline carries 80% of that job.

| Constraint | Verdict |
|-----------|---------|
| Under 10 words | ✅ Ideal: scannable, punchy, clear |
| 10-15 words | ⚠️ Acceptable if every word earns its place |
| Over 15 words | ❌ Too long: loses attention, dilutes impact |

### Headline + sub-headline pairing

The headline captures attention with the core promise. The sub-headline adds specificity without repeating the headline.

- Headline = outcome or transformation (what they get)
- Sub-headline = mechanism or proof (how or why it works)
- Never repeat the same idea in both lines

---

## The 6 headline types

Every LP headline falls into one of these types. Awareness level and offer decide which.

| Type | Purpose | Best for |
|------|---------|----------|
| 1. Outcome | Promise the end result | Most Aware, Product Aware visitors |
| 2. Problem-agitation | Name and intensify the pain | Problem Aware visitors |
| 3. How-to | Promise a method or process | Solution Aware visitors |
| 4. Proof/statistic | Lead with credibility | Product Aware, skeptical audiences |
| 5. Question | Trigger self-identification | Problem Aware, Unaware visitors |
| 6. Direct offer | State the deal | Most Aware visitors, promotions |

## Type 1: Outcome headlines

**Purpose:** Promise the transformation or end result the visitor wants.

**Formula:** `[Desired outcome] + [specificity or timeframe]`

### Lead Gen examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Close 30% More Deals This Quarter" | "The sales acceleration system trusted by 200+ B2B teams" | Specific outcome (30%), time-bound (quarter), relevant metric (deals) |
| "Get Qualified Leads That Actually Convert" | "Stop wasting budget on leads your sales team ignores" | Addresses quality over quantity, speaks to a real frustration |
| "Double Your Pipeline Without Doubling Your Budget" | "See how we help agencies scale lead gen profitably" | Growth promise with efficiency constraint: appeals to both goals |

### SaaS examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Cut Your Reporting Time by 80%" | "Automated dashboards that update while you sleep" | Specific number (80%), tangible benefit (time saved) |
| "Ship Features 3x Faster" | "The development workflow trusted by 500+ engineering teams" | Outcome-first, social proof in sub-headline |
| "Stop Losing Customers to Churn" | "Predict and prevent cancellations before they happen" | Names the enemy (churn), promises prevention |

### Ecommerce examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Sleep Better Starting Tonight" | "Premium memory foam mattresses, 100-night risk-free trial" | Immediate outcome, risk removal in sub-headline |
| "Run Faster, Recover Sooner" | "Performance running shoes engineered for distance runners" | Dual outcome, specific audience segment |
| "Professional Results at Home" | "Salon-grade hair tools used by 50,000+ stylists" | Outcome + social proof |

## Type 2: Problem-agitation headlines

**Purpose:** Name the visitor's pain point and make it feel urgent.

**Formula:** `[Pain point] + [consequence or amplification]`

### Lead Gen examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Tired of Leads That Ghost Your Sales Team?" | "The qualification system that filters out time-wasters before they reach your inbox" | Names a specific frustration, promises a solution mechanism |
| "Your Competitors Are Stealing Your Best Prospects" | "While you wait, they bid. Here's how to win them back" | Creates urgency through competitive threat |
| "Spending €10K/Month on Ads With Nothing to Show?" | "Most agencies waste 40% of ad spend. We fix the leaks" | Specific amount creates recognition, stat adds credibility |

### SaaS examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Still Building Reports Manually?" | "Your team spends 15 hours a week on work that should take 15 minutes" | Time contrast (15 hours vs 15 minutes) quantifies the pain |
| "Your Data Is Scattered Across 12 Tools" | "One dashboard. Every metric. Zero tab-switching" | Specific number (12), clear solution in sub-headline |

### Ecommerce examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Tired of Mattresses That Sag After 6 Months?" | "Our 10-year warranty means you sleep well for a decade" | Specific timeframe (6 months), contrasted with warranty (10 years) |
| "Another Day, Another Bad Hair Day" | "The styling tool that works in 3 minutes flat" | Relatable daily frustration, specific time promise |

## Type 3: How-to headlines

**Purpose:** Promise a method, process, or system that delivers the outcome.

**Formula:** `How to [desired outcome] + [constraint or qualifier]`

### Lead Gen examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "How to Generate 50+ Qualified Leads Per Month" | "The 3-step system that eliminated our client's pipeline anxiety" | Specific volume (50+), specific method (3-step), emotional payoff |
| "How to Scale Google Ads Without Blowing Your Budget" | "The framework 200+ agencies use to grow profitably" | Addresses the growth/efficiency tension directly |

### SaaS examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "How to Reduce Customer Churn by 40% in 90 Days" | "Without discounting, begging, or overhauling your product" | Specific result, time-bound, removes objections in sub-headline |
| "How to Build Dashboards Your Team Actually Uses" | "Drag-and-drop reporting in under 5 minutes" | Addresses the real problem (unused dashboards), easy mechanism |

### Ecommerce examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "How to Get Barista-Quality Coffee at Home" | "The machine that 12,000 coffee lovers swear by" | Aspirational outcome, social proof in sub-headline |

## Type 4: Proof/statistic headlines

**Purpose:** Lead with a credibility-building number or result.

**Formula:** `[Specific statistic or result] + [context]`

### Lead Gen examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "253% More Conversions in 90 Days" | "The landing page framework that transformed our client's pipeline" | Specific percentage, specific timeframe, implies methodology |
| "4,200 Deals Closed Using This System" | "Join the agencies that stopped guessing and started scaling" | Large number creates authority, "join" creates belonging |

### SaaS examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Trusted by 10,000+ Teams Worldwide" | "The project management tool rated #1 on G2 for 3 years running" | Scale (10,000+), third-party validation (G2), longevity (3 years) |
| "99.9% Uptime, Zero Excuses" | "Enterprise-grade reliability at startup-friendly pricing" | Technical proof, addresses two concerns (reliability + cost) |

### Ecommerce examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Rated 4.9/5 by 23,000 Customers" | "The skincare routine dermatologists recommend most" | Volume (23,000) + expert endorsement |
| "50,000 Runners Can't Be Wrong" | "The shoe that won Runner's World Best in Class" | Social proof + authority endorsement |

## Type 5: Question headlines

**Purpose:** Trigger self-identification and curiosity.

**Formula:** `[Question that the target audience answers "yes" to]`

### Lead Gen examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Ready to Stop Losing Deals to Faster Competitors?" | "Speed-to-lead automation that responds in under 60 seconds" | Yes/no self-selection, specific mechanism in sub-headline |
| "What If Every Lead Your Sales Team Called Was Pre-Qualified?" | "AI-powered lead scoring that filters before your team dials" | Hypothetical that paints the ideal state |

### SaaS examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "What Would You Do With 15 Extra Hours Per Week?" | "Automate the reports. Focus on the strategy" | Quantifies the benefit as free time, resonates with time-poor users |

### Ecommerce examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Still Waking Up With Back Pain?" | "Join 30,000 people who fixed their sleep with one mattress change" | Self-identification, social proof in sub-headline |

---

## Type 6: Direct offer headlines

**Purpose:** State the deal clearly for ready-to-buy visitors.

**Formula:** `[Offer] + [key differentiator or urgency]`

### Lead Gen examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Free Google Ads Audit: Find Your Wasted Spend" | "15-minute analysis. Zero commitment. Actionable recommendations" | Clear offer (free audit), removes risk, sets expectations |
| "Get Your Custom Growth Plan in 24 Hours" | "Book a strategy call. We build your roadmap live" | Specific deliverable, fast turnaround, live interaction |

### SaaS examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Start Free for 14 Days. No Credit Card Required" | "Full access to every feature. Cancel anytime" | Removes every barrier (cost, commitment, risk) |
| "60% Off Annual Plans This Week Only" | "Lock in the lowest price we've ever offered" | Clear discount, time pressure, exclusivity |

### Ecommerce examples

| Headline | Sub-headline | Why it works |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| "Buy 2, Get 1 Free" | "Our bestselling bundle, only available this weekend" | Simple math, clear value, urgency |
| "Free Shipping on Orders Over €50" | "Plus a 60-day return guarantee on everything" | Removes friction (shipping cost), adds risk reversal |

## Message match: headline must mirror the ad

The landing page headline must reflect the ad copy that drove the click. Mismatched headlines cause immediate bounces.

| Ad copy | Matching LP headline | Mismatched LP headline |
|---------|---------------------|----------------------|
| "Get a Free Google Ads Audit" | "Your Free Google Ads Audit Starts Here" | "Welcome to Our Marketing Agency" |
| "50% Off Running Shoes" | "50% Off: Premium Running Shoes" | "Shop Our Collection" |
| "Cut Your Reporting Time by 80%" | "Cut Reporting Time by 80% With Automated Dashboards" | "The #1 Analytics Platform" |

> ⚠️ **Keyword match matters for Search traffic:** An ad triggered on "best CRM for small business" needs "CRM" and "small business" in the headline. Dynamic text replacement matches the exact query, see [Dynamic Text Reference](../references/Dynamic Text Reference.md).
