## CTA fundamentals

### Four CTA principles

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| **Value-focused** | Emphasize what they get | Describe what they do |
| **First-person** | "Get my free assessment" | "Get your free assessment" |
| **Specific** | Include what happens next | Use vague action words |
| **Contrasting** | Button visually pops | Button blends into page |

### Primary vs secondary CTAs

| | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|--|-------------|---------------|
| **Purpose** | Drive the main conversion action | Offer a lower-commitment alternative |
| **Prominence** | Largest, most contrasting button | Smaller, less prominent |
| **Commitment** | Higher (buy, sign up, book) | Lower (download, watch, learn more) |
| **Usage** | Always present, repeated throughout | Optional, use sparingly (max 1) |

> ⚠️ **One primary CTA per page:** Multiple competing CTAs split attention and reduce conversions. If you use a secondary CTA, it must still advance toward the primary goal (e.g., "Download 2 free chapters" when primary is "Buy the book").

## The 5 CTA types

| Type | Purpose | Best for |
|------|---------|----------|
| 1. Value-first | Lead with what they receive | High-value offers, free trials, consultations |
| 2. Action-driven | Direct command with benefit | Ecommerce purchases, simple signups |
| 3. Risk-removal | Neutralize hesitation | High-ticket offers, skeptical audiences |
| 4. Urgency-based | Create time pressure | Promotions, limited offers, launch events |
| 5. Low-commitment | Reduce friction with easy first step | Cold traffic, complex solutions, long sales cycles |

---

## Type 1: Value-first CTAs

**Purpose:** Emphasize what the visitor receives, not what they do.

**Formula:** `Get/Claim/Access + [specific deliverable]`

### Lead Gen examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Get My Free Audit" | "Takes 15 minutes. Zero commitment". | Specific deliverable (audit), risk removal in microcopy |
| "Claim My Growth Plan" | "Custom recommendations for your business" | First-person ownership ("my"), personalized promise |
| "Access the Framework" | "The same system used by 200+ agencies" | Social proof in microcopy reinforces value |

### SaaS examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Start My Free Trial" | "No credit card required. Cancel anytime". | Double risk removal (no payment, easy exit) |
| "Get My Demo" | "See it in action. 15 minutes, live walkthrough". | Sets clear expectation on what happens next |
| "Access All Features Free" | "14 days of full access. No strings attached". | Full access promise removes "limited trial" concern |

### Ecommerce examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Get Mine Now" | "Free shipping on all orders" | Urgency ("now") + friction removal (free shipping) |
| "Claim My Discount" | "Save 20% today only" | First-person + urgency |
| "Get My Bundle" | "Save €50 when you buy the set" | Specific savings amount motivates action |

## Type 2: Action-driven CTAs

**Purpose:** Clear, direct commands paired with a benefit.

**Formula:** `[Action verb] + [benefit or object]`

### Lead Gen examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Book My Strategy Call" | "30 minutes. Actionable takeaways guaranteed". | Specific time commitment, outcome promise |
| "Schedule My Consultation" | "Pick a time that works for you" | Reduces friction (they choose timing) |
| "Send Me the Proposal" | "Customized to your business in 24 hours" | Fast turnaround promise |

### SaaS examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Create My Account" | "Setup takes less than 2 minutes" | Speed promise reduces perceived effort |
| "Try It Free" | "Join 10,000+ teams already using it" | Social proof drives confidence |
| "Watch the Demo" | "3-minute overview. No signup required". | Low commitment (no signup), specific duration |

### Ecommerce examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Add to Cart" | "In stock. Ships today". | Availability confirmation + speed |
| "Buy Now" | "60-day money-back guarantee" | Direct action + risk removal |
| "Shop the Collection" | "New arrivals added weekly" | Discovery framing for browsing intent |

## Type 3: Risk-removal CTAs

**Purpose:** Neutralize the fear that stops the visitor from acting.

**Formula:** `[Action] + [guarantee or safety language]`

### Lead Gen examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Try It Risk-Free" | "Cancel anytime. No long-term contracts". | Addresses the "what if I'm stuck" objection |
| "Get Started With Zero Risk" | "If it doesn't work, you don't pay. Period". | Absolute guarantee removes all downside |
| "Book My Free Consultation" | "No sales pressure. Just answers". | Addresses consultation aversion |

### SaaS examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Start Free, Upgrade Later" | "No credit card. No commitment. No catch". | Triple negation of risk |
| "Try Before You Buy" | "Full access for 14 days. Decide after". | Decision delay reduces pressure |

### Ecommerce examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Try It for 100 Nights" | "Don't love it? Full refund, free return pickup". | Extended trial + effortless return |
| "Order With Free Returns" | "Changed your mind? Send it back, no questions asked". | Pre-answers the return concern |

---

## Type 4: Urgency-based CTAs

**Purpose:** Create legitimate time pressure to trigger immediate action.

**Formula:** `[Action] + [deadline or scarcity signal]`

### Lead Gen examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Claim My Spot" | "Only 5 slots left this month" | Scarcity (5 slots) creates urgency |
| "Register Before Friday" | "Early bird pricing ends at midnight" | Specific deadline |
| "Lock In My Rate" | "Price increases January 1st" | Loss aversion (current price disappears) |

### SaaS examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Get 60% Off Now" | "Annual plan discount ends in 37 hours" | Specific countdown creates pressure |
| "Join the Beta" | "Limited to 500 users. 127 spots remaining". | Exact numbers feel authentic |

### Ecommerce examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Buy Before It's Gone" | "Only 3 left in stock" | Real inventory scarcity |
| "Get Today's Deal" | "This price expires at midnight" | Time-bound offer |
| "Order for Christmas Delivery" | "Last order date: December 18th" | External deadline (shipping cutoff) |

> ⚠️ **Only use legitimate urgency:** Fake countdown timers and artificial scarcity destroy trust. If the "limited offer" resets every visit, visitors notice.

---

## Type 5: Low-commitment CTAs

**Purpose:** Reduce friction with an easy first step for hesitant visitors.

**Formula:** `[Low-effort action] + [immediate value]`

### Lead Gen examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "Download the Free Guide" | "47 pages of actionable strategies. No signup required". | Free + no signup = minimal friction |
| "Watch the Case Study" | "See how we generated 3x pipeline in 90 days" | Specific result, video requires zero effort |
| "Get the Checklist" | "The same pre-campaign checklist we use for every client" | Practical tool, implies expertise |

### SaaS examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "See How It Works" | "2-minute product tour. No signup". | Short time commitment, no barrier |
| "Calculate My Savings" | "Enter 3 numbers, get your ROI estimate" | Interactive, personalized, low effort |
| "Read the Comparison" | "Side-by-side: us vs competitors" | Helps decision without commitment |

### Ecommerce examples

| CTA button text | Microcopy | Why it works |
|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| "See It in Action" | "Watch the 60-second demo video" | Video is passive, low effort |
| "View Full Details" | "Ingredients, sizing, reviews" | Information-seeking, not purchasing |
| "Take the Quiz" | "Find your perfect match in 60 seconds" | Interactive, personalized, fun |

## CTA placement strategy

Each CTA pairs with the conversion element that precedes it.

| Placement | Pair with | CTA type |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Hero section | Core value proposition | Value-first or Action-driven |
| After benefits | Value reinforcement | Value-first |
| After social proof | Credibility reminder | Action-driven or Value-first |
| After objection handling | Guarantee statement | Risk-removal |
| After urgency/scarcity | Deadline reminder | Urgency-based |
| Final section | Full value recap | Value-first (strongest CTA) |

### Secondary CTA placement

| Scenario | Secondary CTA | Placement |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| High-ticket Lead Gen | "Download the case study" | After benefits section |
| SaaS with long sales cycle | "Watch the demo" | Hero section (below primary) |
| Ecommerce product page | "Add to wishlist" | Near primary add-to-cart |

## Microcopy patterns

Supporting text near CTAs that reinforces the conversion decision.

| Pattern | Examples | Best paired with |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Risk removal** | "No credit card required" / "Cancel anytime" / "60-day money-back guarantee" | Risk-removal and value-first CTAs |
| **Social proof** | "Join 10,000+ marketers" / "Rated 4.8/5 by 2,300 users" | Action-driven CTAs |
| **Speed/ease** | "Takes 2 minutes" / "Setup in 3 clicks" / "Ships today" | Action-driven and value-first CTAs |
| **Urgency** | "Only 7 spots left" / "Offer ends in 14 hours" | Urgency-based CTAs |
| **Value reinforcement** | "Includes all 300 templates" / "Save €200 today" | Value-first CTAs |
