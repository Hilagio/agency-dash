## Purpose

This mental model gives you the complete methodology for building landing pages that convert. It provides the step-by-step framework for turning an irresistible offer into a deployed, high-converting page.

> ❓ **The core question:** How do I systematically build a landing page that turns the right visitors into leads, orders, or trials?

Your landing page is your 24/7 salesperson. The Conversion Amplifier Framework ensures you build it in the right order, so the foundation is solid before you optimize the surface.

## The Conversion Amplifier Framework

The framework has five steps executed in strict order. Each step depends on the one before it.

```
1. Irresistible Offer
   ↓
2. Page Hierarchy (Structure)
   ↓
3. Copy (Persuasive Writing)
   ↓
4. Design (Visual Hierarchy)
   ↓
5. Build (Implementation)
```

> ⚠️ **Never skip ahead:** Writing copy before defining the page hierarchy leads to unfocused pages. Designing before copy is written leads to layouts that don't serve the message. Building before design is finalized leads to costly rebuilds.

## Step 1️⃣: Irresistible Offer

Everything starts with the offer. If the offer isn't compelling, no amount of copy, design, or traffic fixes the landing page.

### The Irresistible Offer Formula

| Component | Purpose | Test question |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| **Clear Value Proposition** | Communicate what you do and why it matters, instantly | Can a stranger understand this in 5 seconds? |
| **Unique Selling Points** | Differentiate from every alternative | Why you and not the next option? |
| **Value Boosters** | Increase perceived value beyond the core offer | What extras make this feel like a steal? |
| **Social Proof** | Show others have succeeded with this | Why should I believe these claims? |
| **Risk Removal** | Eliminate every reason not to act | What do I lose if this doesn't work? (Answer: nothing) |

**All five components must be present:** A landing page with a clear value prop but no risk removal will leak conversions from risk-averse visitors. A page with social proof but a vague value prop won't capture attention in the first place.

### The awareness level determines your approach

Different visitors need different landing page approaches based on where they are in their buying journey.

| Awareness level | What they know | LP approach |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Unaware** | Don't know they have a problem | Educate first, long-form, content-heavy |
| **Problem Aware** | Know the problem, not the solution | Agitate the problem, introduce the solution category |
| **Solution Aware** | Know solutions exist, evaluating options | Compare, differentiate, prove your solution is best |
| **Product Aware** | Know your product, not yet convinced | Address specific objections, provide proof |
| **Most Aware** | Ready to buy, need a reason to act now | Direct offer, urgency, streamlined path to conversion |

> 💡 **Google Ads traffic awareness mapping:** Bottom-of-funnel keywords (branded, high-intent commercial) typically bring Product Aware and Most Aware visitors. Generic keywords bring Solution Aware. Upper funnel campaigns bring Problem Aware or Unaware. Match your LP to the traffic source.

## Step 2️⃣: Page Hierarchy (Structure)

The page hierarchy determines the information architecture: what sections appear, in what order, and what question each section answers.

### The LP Hierarchy Blueprint

| Section | Question it answers | Position |
|---------|-------------------|----------|
| **1. Hero** | What exactly is being offered? | Above the fold |
| **2. Benefits** | How will this help me? | First scroll |
| **3. Trust/Authority** | Why should I believe you? | After benefits |
| **4. Social Proof** | What do others say about this? | After trust |
| **5. Objection Handling** | What if it doesn't work? | Middle of page |
| **6. Urgency/Scarcity** | Why should I act now? | Before final CTA |
| **7. Call to Action** | What should I do next? | Repeated throughout, final section |

(See: [LP Hierarchy Mental Model](../mental-models/LP Hierarchy Mental Model.md) for detailed breakdown of each section)

**Key principle:** The hierarchy follows the natural psychological progression of a buying decision. Trust must be established before social proof is effective. Objections must be handled before urgency makes sense. Skip a step and conversions leak.

## Step 3️⃣: Copy (Persuasive Writing)

Copy is the conversation between you and your visitor. The mindset shift: stop selling, start helping.

### Core copy principles

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| **Visitor-first** | Focus on their reality, desires, fears | Talk about your company, features, processes |
| **Outcomes over features** | "Save 5 hours per week" | "AI-powered automation engine" |
| **Their language** | Use words your customers actually say | Use industry jargon or corporate speak |
| **Specific** | "43% average ROI increase in 90 days" | "Great results for our clients" |
| **Active voice** | "Get your free assessment" | "An assessment can be requested" |

### Copywriting frameworks

Four proven frameworks for structuring persuasive copy. Choose based on the situation.

| Framework | Structure | Best for |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **AIDA** | Attention → Interest → Desire → Action | Long-form pages, cold traffic, complex solutions, premium offers |
| **PAS** | Problem → Agitation → Solution | Problem-aware audiences, pain-point-focused offers, emotional triggers |
| **BAB** | Before → After → Bridge | Transformation-focused offers, coaching/consulting, clear before/after stories |
| **Hook-Story-Offer** | Hook → Story → Offer | Narrative-driven pages, personal brands, emotional connection required |

> 💡 **Frameworks are not rigid:** Combine elements across frameworks. A PAS opening with an AIDA middle section is perfectly valid. The frameworks provide structure, not constraints.

## Step 4️⃣: Design (Visual Hierarchy)

Design serves the copy and the page hierarchy. It is not decoration: it guides the visitor's eye and reduces friction.

### Design principles for conversion

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|------------|
| **Visual hierarchy** | Most important elements (headline, CTA) get the most visual weight |
| **White space** | Give elements room to breathe, reduce cognitive load |
| **Contrast** | CTAs pop against the background, key text stands out |
| **Consistency** | Colors, fonts, spacing follow a system, not random choices |
| **Supportive visuals** | Images/videos reinforce the message, never distract from it |
| **Mobile-first** | Design for mobile first, then adapt for desktop |

### Common design mistakes

- Generic stock photos that distract instead of reinforce
- Multiple competing visual focal points (the eye doesn't know where to go)
- CTAs that blend into the page instead of contrasting
- Information-dense sections with no visual breaks
- Inconsistent visual language across sections

## Step 5️⃣: Build (Implementation)

Building is the technical execution. Key considerations for conversion-focused builds.

### Build priorities

| Priority | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **Page speed** | Under 3 seconds load time, optimize images, minimize scripts |
| **Mobile responsiveness** | Full functionality on all devices, no horizontal scroll, tap-friendly CTAs |
| **Message match** | Landing page headline and visuals match the ad that drove the click |
| **Tracking** | Conversion events fire correctly, form submissions tracked, micro-conversions captured |
| **Forms** | Minimum fields necessary, multi-step if needed, clear progress indicators |
| **One page, one goal** | Remove navigation, footer links, social icons: eliminate conversion leaks |

### Message match and visual match

Every landing page must match its traffic source:

- **Keyword match:** Headline reflects the search query (use dynamic text replacement where possible)
- **Ad copy match:** Landing page language mirrors the ad's promises and descriptions
- **Visual match:** Display/video ad visuals appear on the landing page for recognition
- **Offer match:** What the ad promises is what the page delivers, no bait-and-switch

---

## The one-page-one-goal rule

> 💡 **Every link that isn't your primary CTA is a conversion leak:** Remove navigation bars, footer links, social media icons, and competing CTAs. One page, one goal, one action.

Exceptions:

- **Anchor navigation** linking to sections on the same page (keeps visitors on-page)
- **One secondary CTA** for a lower-commitment alternative that still advances toward the goal (e.g., "Download 2 free chapters" when the primary CTA is "Buy now")
- **Legal links** required by regulation (open in new tab)

## Practical application

### For Lead Gen / SaaS

The offer drives everything. Start with:
1. Define the irresistible offer (what do they get? why is it valuable? what's the risk removal?)
2. Build the hierarchy around the awareness level of your traffic
3. Write copy that speaks to their specific business pain
4. Design clean, professional pages that build trust
5. Build with fast load times and minimal form fields

**Key focus:** Hero section clarity, trust/authority elements (credentials, case studies), and objection handling around pricing and implementation.

### For Ecommerce

Product pages and category pages function differently from dedicated landing pages:
1. The "offer" is the product itself plus any promotions/bundles
2. Hierarchy prioritizes product imagery, reviews, and purchase friction reduction
3. Copy focuses on benefits over features, with specific use-case examples
4. Design emphasizes product photography and clear add-to-cart paths
5. Build prioritizes page speed and mobile checkout flow

**Key focus:** Product imagery, social proof (reviews), urgency (stock levels, time-limited offers), and reducing checkout friction.

> ↪️ **Ecommerce has its own framework:** See [Ecommerce Conversion Engine Mental Model](../mental-models/Ecommerce Conversion Engine Mental Model.md) for the standalone ecommerce conversion framework with 6 page types, the Ecommerce Persuasion Sequence, and ecommerce-specific conversion principles.

## Key principles

1. **Offer first, always:** If the offer isn't irresistible, nothing else matters. Fix the offer before optimizing the page.
2. **Follow the order:** Offer → Hierarchy → Copy → Design → Build. Each step depends on the previous one.
3. **Match traffic to page:** Different awareness levels need different page approaches. Don't send cold traffic to a "Buy now" page.
4. **One page, one goal:** Remove everything that doesn't serve the primary conversion action.
5. **80% of impact is above the fold:** 80% of visitors decide to stay or leave based on the hero section alone. Invest disproportionate effort there.
