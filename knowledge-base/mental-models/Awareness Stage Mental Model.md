## Purpose

This mental model helps you match your message to your audience's state of mind.

> ❓ **The core question:** How much does your audience already know, and what do they need to hear next?

The same offer requires **different messages** depending on where the audience is in their awareness journey. A "Most Aware" buyer wants the deal. An "Unaware" prospect doesn't even know they have a problem yet.

## The 5 Awareness Stages

| **Stage** | **What they know** | **What they need** | **Temperature** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1️⃣ Unaware** | Doesn't know the problem exists | Problem identification | ❄️ Cold |
| **2️⃣ Problem Aware** | Knows the problem, not the solutions | Problem validation + consequence | ❄️ Cold |
| **3️⃣ Solution Aware** | Knows solutions exist, evaluating options | Your unique approach | 🌤️ Warm |
| **4️⃣ Product Aware** | Knows your product, comparing to alternatives | Specific benefits + differentiation | 🌤️ Warm |
| **5️⃣ Most Aware** | Ready to buy, needs the right offer | Offer details + urgency | 🔥 Hot |

## Message strategy by stage

### 1️⃣ Unaware

*"Did you know this problem exists?"*

| **Element** | **Approach** |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Problem identification: make them aware something is wrong |
| **Tone** | Informative, eye-opening |
| **Lead angle** | Problem/Pain (educational framing) |
| **CTA type** | "Learn more" / "Discover" |

**Example (HeadshotPro):**

"Did you know a poorly taken headshot can impact your professional image and cost you opportunities?"

**Asset implications:**

- Text: Problem-focused headlines
- Images: Problem visualization (before/after, consequences)
- Video: Educational content

---

### 2️⃣ Problem Aware

*"We understand your struggle".*

| **Element** | **Approach** |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Problem articulation: validate their frustration, show consequences |
| **Tone** | Empathetic, understanding |
| **Lead angle** | Problem/Pain (validation framing) |
| **CTA type** | "Discover solution" / "See how" |

**Example (HeadshotPro):**

"We understand the struggle of getting a professional headshot: traditional photoshoots are expensive, time-consuming, and inconvenient".

**Asset implications:**

- Text: Problem validation headlines
- Images: Relatable situations (busy professional, frustrated user)
- Video: Problem stories, "sound familiar?" content

---

### 3️⃣ Solution Aware

*"Here's a better way".*

| **Element** | **Approach** |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Your unique approach: differentiate from other solutions |
| **Tone** | Authoritative, differentiating |
| **Lead angle** | USP / Value Proposition |
| **CTA type** | "See how it works" / "Compare" |

**Example (HeadshotPro):**

"Here's a better way: Get AI-generated professional headshots for 8x less than a physical photo shoot. No studio or appointments needed!"

**Asset implications:**

- Text: Unique approach headlines, "how it works"
- Images: Solution comparison, process visualization
- Video: How it works, demo

---

### 4️⃣ Product Aware

*"Why we're your best choice".*

| **Element** | **Approach** |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Specific benefits & features: why you beat alternatives |
| **Tone** | Confident, specific |
| **Lead angle** | USP + Social Proof |
| **CTA type** | "Compare options" / "See features" |

**Example (HeadshotPro):**

"Why choose HeadshotPro? With AI-powered realism, full customization options, and commercial usage rights, we deliver 200+ headshots faster and for 8x less cost than traditional photoshoots".

**Asset implications:**

- Text: Specific benefit headlines, feature callouts
- Images: Product results, output gallery
- Video: Product demo, feature walkthrough

---

### 5️⃣ Most Aware

*"Here's the deal".*

| **Element** | **Approach** |
| --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Offer details & urgency: give them the deal, remove final friction |
| **Tone** | Direct, urgent |
| **Lead angle** | Value Proposition + Risk Removal + Urgency |
| **CTA type** | "Get started now" / "Buy now" / "Start free trial" |

**Example (HeadshotPro):**

"Ready to upgrade your profile picture? Get 200+ professional headshots starting at €29, delivered within just 2 hours. Satisfaction guaranteed!"

**Asset implications:**

- Text: Offer-focused headlines, price/deal callouts
- Images: Offer visualization, what they get
- Video: Quick offer summary, direct CTA

## Mapping traffic sources to awareness

Different traffic sources typically correlate with awareness stages. The "Temperature" column maps to the [Audience Strategy Mental Model](../mental-models/Audience Strategy Mental Model.md) temperature framework, which uses the same hot → cold spectrum to prioritize audiences by conversion probability.

| **Traffic Source** | **Typical Stage** | **Why** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand Search** | Most Aware | They searched for you by name |
| **Competitor Search** | Product Aware | Comparing options |
| **Product/Service Search** | Solution Aware | Looking for a solution |
| **Problem Search** | Problem Aware | Researching their issue |
| **Display/YouTube prospecting** | Unaware → Problem Aware | Interruption-based, cold |
| **Remarketing** | Product Aware → Most Aware | Already engaged |
| **Email list** | Product Aware → Most Aware | Already opted in |

> 💡 **Search campaigns typically reach Solution Aware → Most Aware audiences.**
> Someone searching "CRM software" already knows they need a CRM. You don't need to educate them on why CRM matters.
> 💡 **Display/Video campaigns often reach Unaware → Problem Aware audiences.**
> You're interrupting them. They weren't looking for you.

## Angle prioritization by stage

Your 6 message angles (from [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md)) should be **weighted differently** based on awareness stage:

| **Angle** | **Unaware** | **Problem Aware** | **Solution Aware** | **Product Aware** | **Most Aware** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem/Pain** | ★★★ | ★★★ | ★★ | ★ | — |
| **Value Proposition** | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ |
| **USPs** | — | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ |
| **Value Boosters** | — | — | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ |
| **Social Proof** | — | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ | ★★ |
| **Risk Removal** | — | — | ★ | ★★ | ★★★ |

**★★★** = Lead with this | **★★** = Include | **★** = Light touch | **—** = Skip

## Practical application

### For Search campaigns (Solution → Most Aware)

Default RSA distribution should emphasize:

- Value Proposition (what you offer)
- USPs (why you're different)
- Social Proof (why trust you)
- Risk Removal (why act now)

Problem/Pain can be used but isn't the lead. **They already know the problem.**

### For Display/Demand Gen (Unaware → Problem Aware)

Lead with:

- Problem/Pain (identify and validate the problem)
- Value Proposition (introduce the solution concept)

Don't lead with offer details. **They're not ready.**

### For Remarketing (Product → Most Aware)

Lead with:

- Value Boosters (remind them what they get)
- Risk Removal (overcome final hesitation)
- Urgency (if authentic)

They already know you. **Close the deal.**

## The message ≠ offer distinction

|  | **Offer** | **Message** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | The facts: what they get | The framing: why they should care |
| **Example** | "100% money-back guarantee" | "Love your headshots or it's free" |
| **Focus** | Deliverables, terms, specs | Emotional triggers, benefits, angles |

> 💡 **Sometimes the offer element outperforms the message.**
> "100% money-back guarantee" might beat "Get headshots you'll love, or get your money back". **Test both. Let data decide.**

---

## Key principles

1. **Meet them where they are:** Don't sell to someone who doesn't know they have a problem. Don't educate someone who's ready to buy.
2. **Match message to stage:** The same offer needs different framing for different stages.
3. **Traffic source = stage signal:** Use traffic source as a proxy for awareness when you can't measure directly.
4. **Angle priority shifts:** Problem/Pain leads for cold traffic. Offer details lead for hot traffic.
5. **One RSA can't do everything:** If you're targeting multiple awareness stages, you may need different ad groups or campaigns with different messaging strategies.
