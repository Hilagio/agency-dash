## Purpose

This SOP guides you through a quarterly business review (QBR) that assesses strategic direction, validates goals and structure, retrospects on constraint sprints, and produces a next-quarter roadmap.

> ❓ **The big question:** Are we solving the right problems with the right structure, and do our goals still reflect business reality?

Quarterly reviews are not extended monthly reviews. Monthly reviews calibrate baselines and catch drift. Quarterly reviews question the goals, structure, and strategy themselves. This is where you decide if the engine is pointed in the right direction, not just running smoothly.

---

## What this SOP is NOT

This SOP does **not:**

- Replace monthly performance reviews (See: [SOP – Run a Monthly Performance Review](../sops/SOP – Run a Monthly Performance Review.md))
- Cover daily or weekly monitoring (See: [SOP – Run a Daily Account Health Check](../sops/SOP – Run a Daily Account Health Check.md))
- Serve as a client presentation template (adapt the QBR document output to your reporting format)
- Provide campaign-level operational procedures (monthly and weekly SOPs handle execution)

## When to run this SOP

Run this SOP:

- First two weeks of each quarter
- After major business pivots (new product, pricing overhaul, market entry)
- During annual planning cycles
- When stakeholders request strategic reassessment

## Phase 2️⃣: Strategy and Structure Review (45 min)

### 2.1 Goal equation validation

Review whether current targets still make sense given business reality:

| Question | Current state | Action needed? |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| Growth targets still realistic? | ___ | Yes / No |
| Efficiency targets still appropriate? | ___ | Yes / No |
| Business model changed? (pricing, margins, capacity) | ___ | Yes / No |
| Market conditions shifted? (macro, demand, regulation) | ___ | Yes / No |
| Stakeholder priorities changed? | ___ | Yes / No |

If any answer is "Yes", document the change and its impact on goals in the QBR document (Phase 4.4).

### 2.2 Account structure assessment

Evaluate whether the current account structure serves the strategy:

1. List all active campaigns with quarterly spend and conversions
2. Identify campaigns with fewer than 30 conversions per month (90 per quarter): candidates for consolidation
3. Identify segments growing fast enough to warrant dedicated campaigns
4. Check for orphaned entities: campaigns, ad groups, or assets running without active oversight
5. Assess whether the campaign structure reflects current business priorities (not legacy priorities from 6+ months ago)

### 4.5 Stakeholder communication

1. Share the QBR document with relevant stakeholders
2. Schedule a review meeting if the QBR reveals significant strategy changes
3. Confirm alignment on next quarter's goals, constraint focus, and budget
4. Document any stakeholder decisions or open items

> ↪️ **QBR presentation structure:** For client-facing QBRs, align with the four-slide structure: Status Board overview, Constraint story (what we worked on and what moved), Backlog (ideas in queue), Next Sprint proposal. This SOP is complete when:

- [ ] Quarter-over-quarter and YoY performance assessed
- [ ] Constraint sprint retrospective completed
- [ ] Goals, structure, channel mix, and technology stack validated
- [ ] Competitive landscape assessed at quarterly trend level

## Exit → Entry bridge

After quarterly review:

| Timeframe | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| This week | Share QBR document, confirm stakeholder alignment |
| Month 1 | Execute structural changes, begin first sprint on new constraint |
| Month 2 | Mid-quarter check: is the constraint moving? |
| Month 3 | Prepare data for next QBR |

**If significant issues found:**

| Issue | Route to |
|-------|----------|
| Goals no longer viable | Business stakeholder meeting for target revision |
| Structure needs major changes | Dedicated restructuring sprint |
| Tracking/attribution broken | Immediate measurement investigation |
| Competitive landscape shifted dramatically | Strategy workshop with stakeholders |

## Common failures

| Failure | Why it happens | How to avoid |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| Quarterly review becomes an extended monthly review | Not shifting from operational to strategic mindset | Focus on goals, structure, and direction, not campaign-level metrics |
| Skipping the constraint retrospective | "We know what we worked on" | Document every sprint formally: patterns emerge only in writing |
| Not questioning goals | Assuming targets from 3 months ago are still valid | Force a goal validation conversation every quarter |
| No stakeholder involvement | Treating QBR as an internal exercise | Quarterly reviews require business context only stakeholders have |
| QBR takes a full day | Trying to redo 3 months of analysis | Time-box to 3 hours. Use monthly reviews as inputs, not raw data |
| No documented output | Review happens verbally, insights are lost | Use the QBR document template every time |
