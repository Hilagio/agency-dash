### Purpose

Expected CTR answers one question only:

> ❓ **The big question:** Is your ad more or less likely to get clicked than your competitors' ads for this keyword (after normalizing for position and visibility factors)?

"Below Average" means Google predicts your ad will get clicked less often than competing ads. This is a **baseline competitiveness problem**.

This SOP **diagnoses** why your Expected CTR is low and **routes you** to the right foundational SOPs for execution.

---

### What this SOP is NOT

This SOP does **not**:

- Fix semantic mismatch between keywords and ads (See: [SOP – Improve Ad Relevance](../sops/SOP – Improve Ad Relevance.md))
- Optimize landing pages (See: [SOP – Improve Landing Page Experience](../sops/SOP – Improve Landing Page Experience.md))
- Run systematic creative testing (See: [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md))
- Teach you how to write headlines (See: [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md))
- Teach you how to write descriptions (See: [Description Expansion Catalog](../catalogs/Description Expansion Catalog.md))

**Key distinction:**

This is a *diagnostic and routing SOP*. The actual creative work happens in the foundational SOPs.

### When to run this SOP

Run this SOP if **ALL** of the following are true:

- Ad Relevance = *Average* or *Above Average* (prerequisite)
- Expected CTR = *Below Average*

> ⚠️ **Do not skip Ad Relevance:** If Ad Relevance is Below Average, your Expected CTR problems may be caused by relevance issues, not creative weakness. Fix relevance first with [SOP – Improve Ad Relevance](../sops/SOP – Improve Ad Relevance.md).

## Phase 1️⃣: Diagnose

### 1.1 The 5-Second Test

Before diving into detailed analysis, run this fast sanity check.

Read your ad out loud. A stranger should be able to answer these questions within 5 seconds:

| Question | If No → Gap |
| --- | --- |
| What do you offer? | Value Proposition missing/weak |
| Why should I care? | Benefit/USP missing/weak |
| Why should I trust you? | Social Proof missing |
| What should I do? | CTA missing/weak |
| Why you over competitors? | Differentiation missing |
| Why act now? | Urgency / Risk Removal missing |

> If your ad fails **2+ questions,** you've already identified your **priority gaps.**

---

### 1.2 RSA baseline checklist

Your RSA must include these elements to compete effectively:

| Element | Question | If Missing → |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relevance anchor** | Does RSA contain the keyword/semantic root? | Run [SOP – Improve Ad Relevance](../sops/SOP – Improve Ad Relevance.md) |
| **Clear Value Prop** | Can a searcher understand what you offer in 3 seconds? | Run [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) → [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md) |
| **Call to Action** | Is there a clear action for the searcher to take? | Run [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md) (CTA section) |
| **Differentiation** | Is there a reason to choose you over competitors? | Run [SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer](../sops/SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer.md) → [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) |
| **Credibility signal** | Is there proof you can deliver? | Run [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) (Social Proof) |
| **Risk reducer** | Is there something that lowers the barrier to action? | Run [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) (Risk Removal) |
| **Description alignment** | Do descriptions reinforce headlines, not repeat them? | Run [Description Expansion Catalog](../catalogs/Description Expansion Catalog.md) |
| **Extension coverage** | Are sitelinks, callouts, images present? | Run [Extension Leverage Catalog](../catalogs/Extension Leverage Catalog.md) |

---

### 1.3 RSA gap analysis

**Action:**

1. Open your RSA in the ad group.
2. List all current headlines and descriptions.
3. Tag each asset with its type:
    - Relevance Anchor
    - Value Proposition
    - Call-to-Action
    - USP / Benefit
    - Social Proof
    - Risk Removal
4. Identify which types are missing or weak.

---

### 1.4 Competitor audit

**Goal:** Understand what you're competing against.

**Action:**

1. Check Auction Insights → **Overlap Rate** to identify top competitors (see screenshot below).
2. Search your main keywords in an incognito browser, or use [**Google's Ads Transparency Center**](https://adstransparency.google.com/).
3. Screenshot the top 3-4 competitor ads.
4. Note what they're doing that you're NOT doing.

![image.png](image.png)

#### What to look for:

| Pattern | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone uses the same angle | Table stakes: you need it too |
| Competitor has angle you're missing | Gap in your RSA |
| You have angle no one else has | Differentiation opportunity: emphasize this |
| Angle is vague across competitors | Opportunity to be specific |

### 1.5 Common gaps that cause "Below Average" Expected CTR

| Gap | Symptom | Root cause SOP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No specific benefit** | Headlines say what you ARE, not what you DO for them | [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) |
| **No social proof** | No ratings, user counts, or credibility signals | [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) |
| **Weak CTA** | "Contact Us" or "Learn More" | [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md) |
| **No differentiation** | Same generic claims as every competitor | [SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer](../sops/SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer.md) |
| **No risk reversal** | Nothing that lowers the commitment barrier | [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) |
| **Self-focused copy** | "We offer..." / "Our solution..." | [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md) |
| **Vague claims** | "Quality solutions", "Great service" | [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md) |
| **No numbers** | Zero quantified claims | [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md) |
| **Descriptions repeat headlines** | Same message in different words | [Description Expansion Catalog](../catalogs/Description Expansion Catalog.md) |
| **Missing extensions** | No sitelinks, callouts, images | [Extension Leverage Catalog](../catalogs/Extension Leverage Catalog.md) |
| **Weak offer** | Nothing compelling to say 💩 | [SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer](../sops/SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer.md) |

## Phase 2️⃣: Route & execute

Based on your gap list, run the appropriate foundational SOPs.

#### Decision Tree

```
Is your OFFER weak or undifferentiated?
│
├─ YES → Run: Create an Irresistible Offer
│         Then: Craft Your Offer Angles
│         Then: Write Headline Assets + Write Description Assets
│
└─ NO → Are your ANGLES documented?
        │
        ├─ NO → Run: Craft Your Offer Angles
        │        Then: Write Headline Assets + Write Description Assets
        │
        └─ YES → Which elements are missing?
                 │
                 ├─ Headlines weak → Run: Write Headline Assets
                 ├─ Descriptions weak → Run: Write Description Assets
                 ├─ Extensions missing → Run: Add Compelling Extensions
                 └─ Multiple gaps → Run all applicable SOPs
```

## Phase 3️⃣: Validate

### 3.1 Monitor Expected CTR

**Timeline:**

- **7 days:** Check for initial CTR movement
- **14-21 days:** Check Expected CTR Quality Score label

#### Validation checklist:

| Timeframe | Metric | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 days | Absolute CTR | Trending upward |
| 14 days | Expected CTR label | No longer "Below Average" |
| 21 days | Expected CTR label | Stable at "Average" or above |

---

### 3.2 If Expected CTR doesn't improve

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| CTR improved but label unchanged | Wait longer (labels lag) |
| CTR flat after 14 days | Re-run Phase 1 diagnosis; something was missed |
| CTR dropped | Check for other changes (bids, competition, landing page) |
| Label stuck at "Average" | "Average" is acceptable; proceed to [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md) |

---

### Validation & definition of done

#### Definition of done

You are finished with this SOP when:

- [ ]  RSA passes the baseline checklist (all elements present)
- [ ]  RSA has clear differentiation from competitors
- [ ]  Extensions are complete and approved
- [ ]  Automated assets are configured correctly
- [ ]  Expected CTR ≠ *Below Average* for **14 consecutive days**

#### Exit → Entry Bridge

Once complete, you have two paths:

- **Path A: Expected CTR = Average, want to optimize further**
    
    → Proceed to [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md)
    
- **Path B: Expected CTR = Average, Landing Page Experience = Below Average**
    
    → Proceed to [SOP – Improve Landing Page Experience](../sops/SOP – Improve Landing Page Experience.md)
    

> ⚠️ **Ongoing Expected CTR improvement** happens through [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md), not through repeated runs of this SOP. This SOP is a one-time diagnostic and repair. The Iteration Loop is the ongoing optimization framework.

### FAQ

**Q: How is this different from the old version of this SOP?**

A: The old version included all the "how to write" content. This version focuses on diagnosis and routes you to modular SOPs for execution. Same outcome, better organization.

**Q: What if I don't know whether my offer is weak?**

A: Run the Offer Audit Checklist in [SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer](../sops/SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer.md). If you score below 8/12, your offer needs work before creative optimization will help.

**Q: Can I skip the foundational SOPs and just write new headlines?**

A: You can, but you'll likely repeat the same mistakes. The foundational SOPs ensure you have the right raw material (angles) before writing.

**Q: What if Expected CTR is "Average": should I still run this?**

A: No. "Average" is acceptable. Proceed directly to [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md) for ongoing optimization.

**Q: How long until Expected CTR improves?**

A: 14-21 days for the label to update. CTR movement appears within the first 7 days.
