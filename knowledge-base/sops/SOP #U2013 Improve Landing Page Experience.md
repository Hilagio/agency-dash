# SOP – Improve Landing Page Experience
Created: 2026-02-04
Updated: 2026-04-02

SOP_ID: SOP_3
Status: Done
Category: Creative
Primary Outcome: Landing Page Experience = Average or Above Average
Secondary Outcomes: Higher conversion rates, lower CPAs, improved Ad Rank
Agent_Executable: No
Human_Approval_Required: No
Domain: Landing Pages
Pillar: 2

### Purpose

Landing Page Experience answers one question:

> ❓ **The big question:** Does your landing page deliver what your ad promised, and does it do so effectively?

"Below Average" means Google has determined that your landing page provides a worse experience than competitors' pages for similar searches. This affects your Quality Score, Ad Rank, and ultimately your CPCs. This SOP fixes **foundational landing page issues** that prevent your pages from meeting Google's quality standards.

> Your landing page is your 24/7 salesperson. Unlike a human who can adapt on the fly, your landing page must be prepared in advance for every visitor scenario.

---

### What Google actually measures

Google evaluates Landing Page Experience (LPE) based on these factors:

| Factor | What Google measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Relevance** | Does the LP content match the ad and keyword? |
| **Usefulness** | Does the LP provide valuable information that answers the searcher's question? |
| **Navigation** | Can visitors easily find what they need? Is the number of links appropriate? |
| **Expectations match** | Does the LP deliver what the ad creative promised? |
| **Mobile-friendliness** | Does the page work well on mobile devices? |
| **Page Speed** | Does the page load quickly enough? |
| **Transparency** | Is the business legitimate? Is contact info available? |

> 💡 **Key insight:** Google uses **conversion rate** as a strong *proxy* for good landing page experience. Pages that convert well signal to Google that visitors found what they were looking for.

---

### What this SOP is NOT

This SOP does **not**:

- Fix ad-to-keyword relevance issues (See: [SOP – Improve Ad Relevance](../sops/SOP – Improve Ad Relevance.md))
- Fix ad creative weakness (See: [SOP – Improve Expected CTR](../sops/SOP – Improve Expected CTR.md))
- Run systematic A/B testing programs (that's ongoing optimization)

**Key distinction:**

This is a *one-time repair* job to fix foundational LP problems. Ongoing landing page optimization is a separate, continuous process.

### When to run this SOP

Run this SOP if **any** of the following are true:

- Landing Page Experience = *Below Average*
- Conversion rates are significantly below industry benchmarks
- Bounce rates are abnormally high (70%+ for most verticals)
- Mobile traffic converts at less than 50% of desktop rate

> Unlike Ad Relevance and Expected CTR, you can run this SOP in parallel with other QS repairs.

### The landing page baseline checklist

Landing Page Experience = Below Average typically means your page fails one or more of these baseline requirements:

| Element | Question | If failing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Message match** | Does the headline match the ad/keyword? | Align LP headline with ad promise |
| **Clear Value Proposition** | Can visitors understand the offer in 5 seconds? | Rewrite hero section |
| **Relevant content** | Does the page answer what the searcher asked? | Add/restructure content |
| **Easy navigation** | Can visitors find what they need without hunting? | Simplify page structure |
| **Mobile-friendly** | Does the page work properly on mobile? | Fix responsive issues |
| **Fast loading** | Does the page load in under 3 seconds? | Optimize page speed |
| **Clear CTA** | Is it obvious what action to take? | Improve CTA visibility |
| **Trust signals** | Are there reasons to trust you? | Add credibility elements |
| **Contact information** | Can visitors reach you if needed? | Add contact details |

> Most Below Average Landing Page Experience cases can be fixed by addressing 2-3 issues from this list.

## Phase 1️⃣: Audit

### 1.1 Message match audit

**Goal:** Verify your landing page delivers what your ad promises.

> ❓ **Core question:** If someone clicks an ad, does the landing page immediately confirm they're in the right place?

**Action:**

1. Pull your top 10 keywords by spend.
2. For each keyword, note the RSA assets that show.
3. Visit the landing page and compare:
    - Does the LP headline reflect the RSA assets?
    - Does the LP content address what the keyword implies?
    - Is the offer mentioned in the ad visible on the LP?

**Message match audit template:**

| Keyword | Ad Headline | LP Headline | Match? | Issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "crm software" | "All-in-One CRM for Sales Teams" | "Welcome to Our Company" | ❌ | LP headline doesn't mention CRM |
| "free crm trial" | "Start Your Free CRM Trial" | "CRM Software - Free Trial" | ✅ | Good match |
| "sales pipeline tool" | "Manage Your Sales Pipeline" | "All-in-One CRM for Sales Teams" | ⚠️ | Partial - mentions sales but not pipeline |

**Common message match failures:**

| Failure | Example | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Generic LP headline** | Ad: "Emergency Plumber 24/7" → LP: "Welcome to ABC Services" | Visitor doesn't know they're in the right place |
| **Different offer** | Ad: "50% Off First Month" → LP: No mention of discount | Visitor feels misled |
| **Wrong product focus** | Ad: "Running Shoes" → LP: General athletic gear page | Visitor has to hunt for what they want |
| **Intent mismatch** | Ad: "Get Instant Quote" → LP: Homepage | Visitor can't take the promised action |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** Use Dynamic Text Replacement (DTR) to automatically match LP headlines to keywords. Most landing page builders (Unbounce, Instapage, Leadpages) have this built in.

---

### 1.2 The 5-Second Test

**Goal:** Verify visitors can understand your offer immediately.

When visitors land on your page, they subconsciously ask these questions in sequence:

| Question | What they're thinking | LP element that answers it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"So what?"** | What is this? What do you offer? | Core value proposition / headline |
| **"Who cares?"** | Why should I care? How does this help me? | Benefits section |
| **"Says who?"** | Why should I believe you? | Trust signals / social proof |
| **"What if...?"** | What if it doesn't work for me? | Objection handling / guarantees |
| **"Why now?"** | Why should I act today? | Urgency / scarcity |
| **"Now what?"** | What should I do next? | Call to action |

**Run the 5-Second Test:**

1. Open your landing page.
2. Look at it for exactly 5 seconds.
3. Look away and answer:
    - What does this company offer?
    - Who is it for?
    - What should I do next?
    - Why should I trust them?

If you can't answer these questions, neither can your visitors.

**Quick audit:**

| Question | If No → |
| --- | --- |
| What do you offer? | Fix Value Proposition (hero section) |
| Why should I care? | Fix Benefits |
| Why should I trust you? | Fix Social Proof |
| What should I do? | Fix CTA |
| Why you over competitors? | Fix Differentiation |
| Why act now? | Fix Urgency / Risk Reversal |

If your LP fails 2+ questions, you've identified your priority gaps.

### 1.3 Technical Audit

**Goal:** Identify technical issues hurting LP Experience.

#### Page Speed Test

1. Go to [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) or download the [Lighthouse Chrome Plugin](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lighthouse/blipmdconlkpinefehnmjammfjpmpbjk)
2. Enter your landing page URL
3. Check both Mobile and Desktop scores

![image.png](image.png)

**Speed benchmarks:**

| Score | Rating | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90–100 | Good | No action needed |
| 50–89 | Needs Improvement | Address major issues |
| 0–49 | Poor | Critical priority |

**Common speed issues:**

| Issue | Impact | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large images | Slow load | Compress images, use WebP format |
| Unoptimized JavaScript | Render blocking | Defer non-critical JS |
| No caching | Repeat load times | Enable browser caching |
| Too many HTTP requests | Slow load | Combine files, reduce plugins |
| No CDN | Geographic latency | Implement CDN |

> ⚠️ **Mobile speed is critical:** For retail, a 1-second delay in mobile load time can impact conversions by up to 20% ([source]<!-- TODO: link target missing -->).

#### Mobile-friendliness test

1. Go to [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) or download the [Lighthouse Chrome Plugin](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lighthouse/blipmdconlkpinefehnmjammfjpmpbjk)
2. Enter your landing page URL
3. Check Mobile scores

**Common mobile issues:**

| Issue | Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text too small | Users must zoom to read | Increase font size (min 16px body) |
| Tap targets too close | Buttons hard to click | Increase button size and spacing (min 44×44px) |
| Horizontal scrolling | Content wider than screen | Fix viewport and responsive CSS |
| Content not visible | Elements hidden on mobile | Check responsive breakpoints |
| Slow mobile load | Takes 5+ seconds | Optimize for mobile-first |

#### Core Web Vitals check

Check these three metrics in PageSpeed Insights:

| Metric | What it measures | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LCP** (Largest Contentful Paint) | How fast main content loads | < 2.5 seconds |
| **INP** (Interaction to Next Paint) | How fast page responds to input | < 200 milliseconds |
| **CLS** (Cumulative Layout Shift) | How much page jumps around | < 0.1 |

### 1.4 Content & navigation audit

**Goal:** Verify your page is useful and easy to navigate.

**Navigation checklist:**

| Element | Check | Issue if failing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary CTA visible** | Can you see the main CTA without scrolling? | CTA buried below fold |
| **Clear information hierarchy** | Is content organized logically? | Confusing structure |
| **Minimal distractions** | Are there competing links/CTAs? | Too many exit points |
| **Scannable content** | Can visitors skim and understand? | Wall of text |
| **Logical flow** | Does content progress naturally? | Disjointed sections |

**Attention Ratio Check:**

> 💡 **Attention Ratio** = Number of things you can do ÷ Number of things you should do

**Ideal:** 1:1 (One page, one purpose, one CTA)

**Audit your page:**

- Count all clickable links (navigation, footer, social, etc.)
- Count CTAs pointing to your conversion goal
- Calculate ratio

| Ratio | Rating | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1:1 to 3:1 | Good | Focused page |
| 4:1 to 10:1 | Needs work | Remove distracting links |
| 10:1+ | Poor | Major restructure needed |

**Common navigation issues:**

| Issue | Impact | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full website navigation on LP | Visitors leak to other pages | Remove nav or use anchor links only |
| Multiple competing CTAs | Decision paralysis | One primary CTA per page |
| Footer links | Exit points | Minimize or remove |
| Social media links | Traffic leakage | Move to thank-you page |
| Excessive internal links | Distraction | Remove non-essential links |

### 1.5 Trust & transparency audit

**Goal:** Verify your page establishes credibility.

Google explicitly looks for transparency and trust signals. Missing these can hurt LP Experience.

**Trust elements checklist:**

| Element | Present? | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Company name visible** | Yes/No | High |
| **Physical address** | Yes/No | High (especially for local) |
| **Phone number** | Yes/No | High (especially for Lead Gen) |
| **Email address** | Yes/No | Medium |
| **Privacy policy link** | Yes/No | High |
| **Terms of service link** | Yes/No | Medium |
| **Social proof** (reviews, testimonials) | Yes/No | High |
| **Trust badges** (security, certifications) | Yes/No | Medium |
| **Real team photos** | Yes/No | Medium |
| **Case studies / results** | Yes/No | Medium |

> 💡 **For Lead Gen especially:** Missing contact information is a major trust killer. If you're asking for someone's information, they need to know you're legitimate.

## Phase 2️⃣: Repair

*Execute this phase for each gap identified in Phase 1.*

> ⚠️ **Can't fix a foundational issue? The problem might be your offer, not your landing page.**
> If you struggle to create a compelling value proposition, can't articulate clear benefits, have no social proof to show, or can't offer any risk reversal, the issue isn't your landing page. **It's your offer.**
> 💡 **A landing page can only be as strong as the offer behind it.**
> Before continuing, ask yourself:
> - Do we have a compelling reason for someone to choose us?
> - Do we have proof that we deliver results?
> - Do we have something that lowers the risk of trying us?
> If the answer is "no" to multiple questions, pause this SOP and fix the offer first. No amount of landing page optimization will compensate for a weak or undifferentiated offer.

### 2.1 Fix message match issues

**Goal:** Ensure your LP delivers exactly what your ad promises.

#### Option A: align LP to Ads (Easier)

If you have a few high-volume keywords, adjust your LP to match:

1. Identify your top 5 keywords by spend
2. Update LP headline to include the core keyword/theme
3. Ensure the offer mentioned in ads is prominent on LP
4. Match the CTA language between ad and LP

**Example:**

| Element | Ad | LP (Before) | LP (After) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | "CRM for Sales Teams" | "Welcome to SalesForce Pro" | "CRM Software for Sales Teams" |
| CTA | "Start Free Trial" | "Get Started" | "Start Your Free Trial" |
| Offer | "Free 14-Day Trial" | (not mentioned) | "Try Free for 14 Days" prominently displayed |

#### Option B: Use Dynamic Text Replacement (Advanced)

For accounts with many keyword variations, implement DTR:

**How DTR Works:**

1. Add URL parameters to your final URLs: `?keyword={keyword}`
2. Landing page (builder) reads the parameter
3. LP headline dynamically updates to match

**Example:**

- URL: [`yoursite.com/crm?keyword=sales+crm+software`](http://yoursite.com/crm?keyword=sales+crm+software)
- LP headline shows: "Sales CRM Software" instead of generic headline

**DTR Benefits:**

- Perfect message match without building multiple pages
- Consolidated traffic for better conversion data
- Faster A/B testing with larger sample sizes

> Most landing page builders (Unbounce, Instapage, Leadpages) have built-in DTR. If you're not using a builder, you can build custom DTR functionality. It’s one of the first thing I (Bob) ask my client’s developers to build for me.

### 2.2 Fix hero section issues

**Goal:** Make your value proposition crystal clear in the first 5 seconds.

The hero section is where 80% of visitor attention goes. If it fails, nothing else matters.

**Hero section elements**

| Element | Purpose | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary headline** | Grab attention, communicate core value | Clear, specific, benefit-focused |
| **Subheadline** | Expand on headline, add detail | Clarifies the "how" or "who" |
| **Hero visual** | Reinforce message, show outcome | Relevant, high-quality |
| **Primary CTA** | Tell visitors what to do | Visible, action-oriented |
| **Trust indicator** | Build immediate credibility | Social proof or authority |

#### Headline formula

**Primary headline:** Focus on the outcome or transformation.

| Vertical | ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead Gen | "Professional Roofing Services" | "Licensed Roofers in [City] - Free Quote in 1 Minute" |
| SaaS | "Powerful Business Software" | "Close Deals 40% Faster with AI-Powered CRM" |
| Ecommerce | "Quality Running Shoes" | "200+ Running Shoes - Free Shipping Over €50" |

#### Subheadline strategies

Choose a strategy that complements your headline:

| Strategy | Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clarifier** | Explain the headline | "Our AI-powered platform analyzes your pipeline and prioritizes deals most likely to close" |
| **Qualifier** | Define who it's for | "For B2B sales teams managing 50+ deals per month" |
| **Risk Reverser** | Remove objections | "No credit card required. Cancel anytime". |
| **Proof Provider** | Add credibility | "Trusted by 50,000+ sales professionals worldwide" |

#### Hero visual guidelines

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Show the end result or transformation | Use generic stock photos |
| Use high-quality, relevant images | Show unrelated "happy people" |
| Show the product in use | Use cluttered or busy images |
| Use real customer photos if possible | Use fake testimonials or ones that can’t be verified |

### 2.3 Fix content & structure issues

**Goal:** Organize information so visitors find what they need.

#### The Hierarchy Blueprint

Structure your page to answer visitor questions in order:

| Section | Question It Answers | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero** | "So what?" | Core value proposition |
| **Benefits** | "Who cares? / How will this help me?" | Key outcomes for the visitor |
| **Trust/Authority** | "Says who? / Why should I believe you?" | Credentials, experience, results |
| **Social Proof** | "What do others say?" | Testimonials, reviews, case studies |
| **Objection Handling** | "What if it doesn't work?" | FAQs, guarantees, risk reversal |
| **Urgency** | "Why should I act now?" | Limited time/availability (if authentic) |
| **CTA** | "Now what?" | Clear next step |

#### Content fixes

**If content is too thin:**

- Add benefit-focused sections
- Include social proof (testimonials, case studies)
- Add FAQ section addressing common concerns
- Include process/how-it-works section

**If content is too dense:**

- Break up walls of text
- Use bullet points for scannable content
- Add visual breaks (images, icons)
- Implement progressive disclosure (expandable sections)

**If content is disorganized:**

- Reorder sections to match the Hierarchy Blueprint
- Ensure each section has a clear purpose
- Remove redundant or off-topic content
- Add section headers for scannability

### 2.4 Fix CTA issues

**Goal:** Make it obvious and easy to take action.

**CTA visibility rules**

| Rule | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| **Above the fold** | Primary CTA visible without scrolling |
| **Repeated appropriately** | CTA appears after key sections (not excessive) |
| **Visually prominent** | Contrasting color, sufficient size |
| **Clear copy** | Benefit-focused, specific action |

**CTA copy by vertical**

| Vertical | ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead Gen | "Submit" | "Get My Free Quote" |
| SaaS | "Sign Up" | "Start My Free 14-Day Trial" |
| Ecommerce | "Buy" | "Add to Cart - Free Shipping" |

**Supporting elements near CTA**

Reinforce your CTA with:

| Element | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Value reminder** | "Join 50,000+ sales professionals" |
| **Risk reversal** | "No credit card required" |
| **Urgency** (if authentic) | "Only 3 spots left this month" |
| **Social proof snippet** | "⭐ 4.9/5 from 2,000+ reviews" |

### 2.5 Fix trust & credibility issues

**Goal:** Give visitors reasons to trust you.

#### Essential trust elements

**For all verticals:**

| Element | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact information** | Phone, email, address visible |
| **Privacy policy** | Link in footer |
| **Social proof** | Reviews, testimonials, user counts |
| **Professional design** | Clean, modern, no obvious errors |

**Vertical-specific trust elements:**

| Vertical | Priority trust elements |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead Gen** | Local phone number, physical address, license numbers, project photos |
| **SaaS** | User count, client logos, G2/Capterra ratings, security badges, uptime stats |
| **Ecommerce** | Review count, secure checkout badges, return policy, shipping info, payment icons |

#### Social Proof types

| Type | Example | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Testimonials** | Quote from customer with photo/name | All verticals |
| **Case studies** | Detailed success story with results | B2B, SaaS |
| **Reviews/ratings** | "4.8/5 from 10,000+ reviews" | Ecommerce, SaaS |
| **User count** | "Trusted by 50,000+ teams" | SaaS |
| **Client logos** | Recognizable company logos | B2B, SaaS |
| **Media mentions** | "As seen in Forbes, TechCrunch" | All verticals |
| **Certifications** | Industry badges, security certs | All verticals |

> 💡 **Make social proof verifiable:** Include full names, company names, photos, and links to original reviews when possible. Unverifiable testimonials create suspicion and and work counterproductively.

## Phase 3️⃣: Validate

### 3.1 Monitor Landing Page Experience

**Goal:** Confirm the repairs improved Landing Page Experience.

**Timeline**

| Timeframe | What to Check |
| --- | --- |
| **7 days** | Conversion rate trending up |
| **14 days** | Check LP Experience label in Google Ads |
| **21–30 days** | LP Experience stable at "Average" or above |

**How to check LP Experience**

1. Google Ads → Keywords
2. Columns → Modify → Quality Score → Landing Page Experience
3. Filter to show "Below Average"

#### Use conversion rate as proxy

Google uses conversion signals as part of LP Experience assessment. Track:

| Metric | Target Trend |
| --- | --- |
| Conversion Rate | Increasing |
| Bounce Rate | Decreasing (not relevant for onepagers) |
| Time on Page | Increasing (for considered purchases) |
| Pages per Session | Context-dependent |

### 3.2 Troubleshooting

**If Landing Page Experience is still Below Average after 30 days:**

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CVR improved but label unchanged | Not enough data or time | Wait longer, ensure sufficient traffic |
| CVR flat | Repairs didn't address the real issue | Re-audit, check competitor LPs |
| CVR declined | New LP is worse | Revert changes, try different approach |
| Mobile CVR still poor | Mobile experience broken | Focus on mobile-specific fixes |
| High bounce rate persists | Message match or relevance issue | Re-audit message match |
| Mixed results across keywords | Different keywords need different LPs | Consider keyword-specific landing pages |

### Validation & definition of done

#### Definition of done

You are finished with this SOP when:

- [ ]  LP passes the 5-Second Test (value prop is clear)
- [ ]  Message match verified between ads and LP
- [ ]  Mobile-friendly click rate = 100%
- [ ]  PageSpeed score ≥ 50 (mobile)
- [ ]  Essential trust elements present
- [ ]  Clear CTA visible above fold
- [ ]  Landing Page Experience ≠ *Below Average* for **14+ consecutive days**

#### Exit → Entry Bridge

Once complete, you have two paths:

- **Path A: LP Experience = Average, other QS components still Below Average**
    
    → Return to [SOP – Improve Ad Relevance](../sops/SOP – Improve Ad Relevance.md) or [SOP – Improve Expected CTR](../sops/SOP – Improve Expected CTR.md)
    
- **Path B: All QS components = Average or better, want to optimize further**
    
    → Proceed to ongoing CRO
    

> ⚠️ **Ongoing LP optimization** happens through systematic CRO and A/B testing, not through repeated runs of this SOP. This SOP is built for one-time repairs.

### Common gaps that cause Below Average Landing Page Experience

| Gap | Symptom | Impact | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **No message match** | LP headline doesn't reflect ad/keyword | Visitors don't know they're in the right place | Align LP headline with ad promise or use DTR |
| **Unclear value proposition** | Can't understand offer in 5 seconds | High bounce rate, low engagement | Rewrite hero section with clear benefit |
| **Slow page load** | PageSpeed score < 50 | Visitors leave before page loads | Compress images, optimize code, use CDN |
| **Poor mobile experience** | Mobile-friendly rate < 100% | Mobile visitors can't convert | Fix responsive issues, increase tap targets |
| **No social proof** | No testimonials, reviews, or trust signals | Visitors don't trust you | Add verifiable social proof elements |
| **Buried CTA** | Primary action not visible above fold | Visitors don't know what to do | Move CTA above fold, increase visibility |
| **Too many distractions** | Navigation, multiple CTAs, exit links | Visitors leak off the page | Remove nav, focus on one CTA, 1:1 attention ratio |
| **Missing contact info** | No phone, email, or address | Visitors question legitimacy | Add contact information visibly |
| **Content doesn't match intent** | Transactional keyword → informational page | Visitors can't complete desired action | Match page type to search intent |
| **Wall of text** | Dense, unformatted content | Visitors won't read | Break up content, add visuals, use headers |
| **Generic stock photos** | Obviously fake or irrelevant images | Reduces trust | Use real photos, product shots, or custom graphics |
| **No risk reversal** | Nothing that lowers commitment barrier | Visitors hesitate to act | Add guarantees, free trials, return policies |

### FAQ

**Q: How is Landing Page Experience different from the other QS components?**

A: Ad Relevance measures keyword-to-ad match. Expected CTR measures ad competitiveness. Landing Page Experience measures whether your landing page delivers value after the click. You can have perfect Ad Relevance and Expected CTR but still fail on Landing Page Experience if your page is slow, confusing, or doesn't match expectations.

**Q: Can I use my website's homepage as a landing page?**

A: Generally, no. Homepages have multiple goals and many exit points. Dedicated landing pages with a single focus (1:1 attention ratio) almost always outperform homepages for paid traffic. Exception: Brand campaigns where visitors expect to land on your homepage.

**Q: How long until Landing Page Experience improves?**

A: Typically 14-30 days for the label to update, assuming sufficient traffic. The more traffic the keyword receives, the faster Google updates the score.

**Q: Should I build separate landing pages for each keyword?**

A: Not necessarily. Use DTR to personalize one page for many keywords. Build separate pages when intent is fundamentally different (e.g., "buy" vs. "compare" keywords).

**Q: What PageSpeed score should I aim for?**

A: Minimum 50 for mobile. Ideally 70+. However, don't sacrifice conversion elements for speed. A slightly slower page that converts better is preferable to a fast page that doesn't convert.

**Q: Does Landing Page Experience affect Display and Video campaigns?**

A: The "Landing Pages" report in Google Ads covers Search, Display, Video, and Shopping campaigns. However, Quality Score (including LP Experience) is primarily a Search concept. For Display and Video, focus on conversion rate as your primary LP metric.
