# SOP – Build a High-Converting Ecommerce Landing Page
Created: 2026-02-04

SOP_ID: SOP_19
Status: Done
Category: Creative
Agent_Executable: No
Human_Approval_Required: Yes
Primary Outcome: No-navigation ecommerce LP with full persuasion sequence, validated and ready for paid traffic
Secondary Outcomes: Higher conversion rate on focused campaigns, controlled A/B testing environment
Domain: Landing Pages
Pillar: 2

## Purpose

Builds a dedicated ecommerce landing page for paid traffic campaigns: promotion pushes, seasonal offers, single-product or bundle campaigns. The page strips navigation, follows the Ecommerce Persuasion Sequence, and uses long-form persuasion to convert cold or warm traffic.

> ❓ **The big question:** How do I build a focused, no-navigation ecommerce LP that converts paid traffic better than sending visitors to the standard product page?

This SOP is for dedicated ecommerce LPs only. For standard product pages (with site navigation, used for Shopping PLAs and organic), see [SOP – Build a High-Converting Product Page](../sops/SOP – Build a High-Converting Product Page.md).

---

## What this SOP is NOT

This SOP does **not:**

- Build standard product pages with site navigation (See: [SOP – Build a High-Converting Product Page](../sops/SOP – Build a High-Converting Product Page.md))
- Build Lead Gen or SaaS landing pages (See: standard LP SOPs)
- Optimize cart or checkout flow (See: [SOP – Optimize Cart and Checkout Flow](../sops/SOP – Optimize Cart and Checkout Flow.md))
- Define the offer: pricing, promotion, and guarantee must be decided before this SOP runs

## When to run this SOP

Run this SOP when:

- Running a specific promotion or seasonal sale that needs a focused destination
- Launching a single-product or bundle campaign with cold traffic (Display, YouTube, Demand Gen)
- You want full control over messaging and want to A/B test independently from the product page
- The standard product page isn't converting well for paid traffic and you need a more persuasive alternative

## Before you start

### Decision gate: LP vs product page

Before building, confirm that a dedicated LP is the right choice.

| If... | Then... |
|-------|---------|
| Running a specific promotion or seasonal offer | Build a dedicated LP (this SOP) |
| Traffic is from Shopping PLAs (expects a product page) | Send to product page, not a dedicated LP |
| Cold traffic from Display/YouTube/Demand Gen | Build a dedicated LP (this SOP) |
| Visitor needs to browse variants or related products | Send to product page |
| Product page already converts well for paid traffic | No LP needed |
| You want to A/B test messaging independently | Build a dedicated LP (this SOP) |

> ⚠️ **Do not use this SOP for Shopping PLA traffic:** Shopping visitors expect a product page with site navigation. A stripped LP will confuse them and increase bounce rate.

### Required inputs

- Product details: name, features, specifications, images/video, pricing
- Offer: the specific promotion, bundle, or pricing structure
- Target audience: who this campaign targets (for messaging angle)
- Customer reviews: best 5-10 reviews with photos (curated)
- Competitive context: what alternatives exist and why yours is better

## Phase 1️⃣: Offer and angle

### 1.1 Define the offer

Document the complete offer that this LP presents. Everything the visitor needs to make a purchase decision must be on this one page.

1. **Product or bundle:** What exactly is being sold? If a bundle, list all included items.
2. **Price and promotion:** Current price, original price, savings, promotional terms (e.g. "30% off until March 15").
3. **Shipping:** Free shipping included? If not, what is the cost?
4. **Guarantee:** Return policy, money-back guarantee, warranty.
5. **Urgency:** What legitimate reason exists to buy now? (stock limit, promotion end date, shipping cutoff)

### 1.2 Define the messaging angle

Based on the traffic source and audience, determine the primary messaging approach.

| Traffic source | Awareness level | Messaging angle |
|---------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Branded search | High awareness | Lead with offer/price, light persuasion |
| Generic product search | Medium awareness | Lead with product benefits, social proof heavy |
| Display/YouTube | Low awareness | Lead with problem/transformation, full persuasion sequence |
| Demand Gen | Low-medium | Lead with lifestyle/aspiration, visual-heavy |
| Retargeting | High (already visited) | Lead with urgency/offer, address remaining objections |

### 1.3 Offer worksheet

| Element | Value | Section on LP |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Product/bundle name | | Product Showcase (section 1) |
| Key differentiator tagline | | Product Showcase (section 1) |
| Price (current) | | Product Showcase (section 1) + Complete Your Purchase (section 7) |

## Phase 2️⃣: Build the Ecommerce Persuasion Sequence

The dedicated ecommerce LP follows the Ecommerce Persuasion Sequence defined in the [Ecommerce Conversion Engine Mental Model](../mental-models/Ecommerce Conversion Engine Mental Model.md). Use the [Ecommerce Page Section Catalog](../catalogs/Ecommerce Page Section Catalog.md) (sections 4.1-4.7) for detailed patterns and examples.

The key structural difference from Lead Gen/SaaS LPs: proof comes before benefits. The product image already communicates what's being offered, so the visitor's next question is "Does it actually work?" not "What does it do?"

### 2.1 Section 1: Product Showcase

The product showcase must answer: "What exactly is being offered?"

1. **Hero visual:** Product image or video as the primary visual (not a headline-first layout, not a lifestyle-only image).
2. **Price visible:** Current price (and savings if discounted) above the fold.
3. **Key differentiator tagline:** One line of product positioning, under 10 words.
4. **Primary CTA:** "Buy now", "Get yours", or "Add to cart" with clear styling.

> 💡 **Message match:** The tagline and offer must mirror the ad that brought the visitor here. If the ad says "50% off running shoes", the LP must say "50% off running shoes", not "Premium athletic footwear".

### 2.2 Section 2: Customer Evidence

Curate the best evidence that the product delivers on its promises. This section comes early because the product image already established what's being offered.

1. **Aggregate proof:** "4.8/5 from 1,200+ reviews" or "50,000+ sold".
2. **Customer reviews:** Select 5-10 best reviews with specific results, photos, and verified buyer badges.
3. **Before/after:** If applicable, show transformation photos with customer permission.
4. **UGC:** Customer photos and videos from social media.

### 2.3 Section 3: Product Benefits

Translate product features into visitor outcomes. Now that the visitor has seen proof the product works, benefit claims land harder.

1. **Lead with outcomes:** "Stay dry in any weather" not "Waterproof membrane".
2. **Tie back to evidence:** "312 customers mentioned comfort in reviews" alongside the comfort benefit.
3. **Use benefit display patterns:** Icon + headline + description cards, or before/after comparisons.
4. **Include lifestyle imagery:** Product being used in real context alongside each benefit.

### 2.4 Section 4: The Details

Provide the information that detail-oriented buyers need.

1. **Specifications:** Materials, dimensions, weight, care instructions in a scannable format.
2. **Comparison:** How this product compares to alternatives (your other products or generic category).
3. **"What's in the box":** List and/or photo of everything included.
4. **How it works/how to use:** Short video or 3-step instruction if applicable.

### 2.5 Section 5: Purchase Confidence

Address the transactional concerns that block ecommerce purchases.

1. **Guarantee detail:** Expand on the return policy: "60-day money-back guarantee. Email us, get a refund within 48 hours. No questions, no forms".
2. **Shipping clarity:** Cost, timeline, and tracking information.
3. **Size/fit guidance:** Size chart, fit guide, or "how to measure" section if applicable.
4. **FAQ section:** 5-7 expandable Q&A items addressing the objections identified in Phase 1.

### 2.6 Section 6: Act Now

Create a legitimate reason to buy now instead of later.

1. **Stock scarcity:** "Only X left at this price" (if genuine).
2. **Promotion deadline:** "Sale ends March 15 at midnight" with countdown timer.
3. **Shipping cutoff:** "Order by 3pm today for delivery by Thursday".

> ⚠️ **Never fake scarcity:** Countdown timers that reset on refresh destroy trust permanently. Only use urgency elements tied to real constraints.

### 2.7 Section 7: Complete Your Purchase

The closing section recaps value and drives the purchase decision.

1. **Value recap:** Summarize the product, key benefits, and what's included.
2. **Price and savings:** Repeat the offer with pricing.
3. **CTA:** Prominent "Buy now" or "Add to cart" button.
4. **Express checkout:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay buttons.
5. **Guarantee reminder:** Final risk removal: "Free shipping. 60-day returns. Satisfaction guaranteed".

## Phase 3️⃣: Copy, design, and checkout flow

### 3.1 Write persuasive copy

This is a long-form persuasion page, not a product catalog page. Apply ecommerce copy principles:

1. **Visitor-first language:** "You" not "We". "Your feet stay dry" not "Our membrane blocks water".
2. **Specific claims:** "Lasts 500+ washes" not "Long-lasting". "€36 savings" not "Great deal".
3. **Multiple CTAs:** Place CTA buttons after the hero, after social proof, and at the bottom (minimum 3).
4. **Transition copy:** Between sections, use bridge sentences that guide the reader forward.

### 3.2 Apply design hierarchy

1. **No navigation:** Strip all header nav, footer links, and exit points. Only the CTA and "back" should be clickable.
2. **Visual flow:** Alternating image-text sections for easy scanning.
3. **CTA contrast:** CTA buttons use the highest-contrast color on the page.
4. **Mobile-first:** Design for 375px viewport first, then expand to desktop.

### 3.3 Configure checkout flow

For dedicated LPs, minimize steps between CTA click and payment:

| Approach | When to use |
|----------|------------|
| Direct-to-checkout (skip cart) | Single product or bundle, no variant selection needed on LP |
| Add-to-cart + skip to checkout | When visitor selects variant on the LP, then goes straight to checkout |
| Standard cart flow | When you expect visitors to add more products (less common on dedicated LPs) |

## Phase 4️⃣: Validate and launch

### 4.1 Run the checklist

Run the dedicated ecommerce LP section of the [Ecommerce Page Quality Checklist](../checklists/Ecommerce Page Quality Checklist.md). Every item must pass.

### 4.2 Test the full flow

1. Open the LP on a real mobile device.
2. Read through the entire page (not just scroll).
3. Click the CTA and complete a test purchase through checkout.
4. Verify tracking fires: page view, add-to-cart, purchase events.
5. Verify no navigation leaks (no header, no footer links, no logo click).

### 4.3 Final checks

- [ ] No navigation elements on the page (no header nav, no footer links)
- [ ] All 7 Ecommerce Persuasion Sequence sections are present in order
- [ ] Price is visible above the fold
- [ ] Minimum 3 CTAs on the page (after hero, after social proof, bottom)
- [ ] Hero headline matches the ad that will drive traffic to this page
- [ ] Urgency elements are legitimate (not fake timers)
- [ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] Full purchase flow works end-to-end
- [ ] Conversion tracking fires correctly

## Exit → Entry bridge

Once the LP is live:

| Next step | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Launch campaign | Point the paid traffic campaign to this LP |
| Monitor performance | Track conversion rate, bounce rate, and time on page for the first 2 weeks |
| A/B test | Test headline, hero image, or offer structure once you have baseline data |
| Iterate | If conversion rate is below target, audit sections using the checklist |

**If issues arise:**

| Issue | Route to |
|-------|----------|
| High bounce rate | Check message match (ad vs LP hero headline) |
| Low conversion despite time on page | Review social proof and objection handling sections |
| Good add-to-cart but low purchase | [SOP – Optimize Cart and Checkout Flow](../sops/SOP – Optimize Cart and Checkout Flow.md) |
