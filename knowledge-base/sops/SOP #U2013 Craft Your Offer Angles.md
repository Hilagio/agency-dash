## Purpose

Great ad copy doesn't come from templates. **It comes from understanding your offer deeply.**

> ❓ **The big question:** What ammunition does your offer give you to work with, and which ammunition matters most for your audience?

This SOP extracts the raw material for your RSA assets and prioritizes it based on who you're talking to. Before writing any headlines or descriptions, you need to know:

1. What angles your offer supports
2. Which angles to emphasize for your traffic

---

## Phase 0️⃣: Classify "Traffic Temperature"

### 0.1 Identify the awareness stage

Before extracting angles, determine **who you're talking to**.

> ❓ **Ask yourself:** When someone sees this ad, how much do they already know?

**Use traffic source as your primary signal:**

| Traffic source | Typical stage | Temperature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand Search | Most Aware | 🔥 Hot |
| Competitor Search | Product Aware | 🔥 Hot |
| Product/Service Search | Solution Aware | 🌤️ Warm |
| Problem/Symptom Search | Problem Aware | ❄️ Cold |
| Display prospecting | Unaware → Problem Aware | ❄️ Cold |
| YouTube prospecting | Unaware → Problem Aware | ❄️ Cold |
| Remarketing | Product → Most Aware | 🔥 Hot |

**Document your classification:**

| Field | Your answer |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign/Traffic Source | e.g., "Non-brand Search: CRM software" |
| Primary Awareness Stage | e.g., "Solution Aware" |
| Traffic Temperature | ❄️ Cold / 🌤️ Warm / 🔥 Hot |

### 0.2 Set angle priorities

Based on your awareness stage, determine which angles to **lead with** vs. **support with**:

| Angle | ❄️ Cold (Unaware/Problem) | 🌤️ Warm (Solution) | 🔥 Hot (Product/Most) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem/Pain** | ★★★ Lead | ★★ Include | ★ Light |
| **Value Proposition** | ★★ Include | ★★★ Lead | ★★ Include |
| **USPs** | ★ Light | ★★★ Lead | ★★★ Lead |
| **Value Boosters** | — Skip | ★★ Include | ★★★ Lead |
| **Social Proof** | ★ Light | ★★ Include | ★★★ Lead |
| **Risk Removal** | — Skip | ★★ Include | ★★★ Lead |
- **★★★ Lead** = Dedicate multiple headline slots
- **★★ Include** = At least one headline slot
- **★ Light** = Optional, in descriptions
- **— Skip** = Don't emphasize

**Document your priorities:**

| Priority level | Angles for your traffic |
| --- | --- |
| Lead with (★★★) | e.g., "Value Proposition, USPs" |
| Include (★★) | e.g., "Social Proof, Risk Removal" |
| Light touch (★) | e.g., "Problem/Pain" |
| Skip (—) | e.g., "Value Boosters" |

> ↪️ **For deeper context on awareness stages**, see: [Awareness Stage Mental Model](../mental-models/Awareness Stage Mental Model.md)

## Phase 2️⃣: Extract your message angles

### The 6 message angles

Every compelling RSA is built from six core message angles. Each angle answers a specific question in the searcher's mind:

| Angle | Question it answers | What to extract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem/Pain Points** | *"Do you understand my struggle?"* | The pain, frustration, or symptom your audience experiences |
| **Value Proposition** | *"What do you offer?"* | Core product/service + main benefit/outcome |
| **USPs** | *"Why are you better/different?"* | Speed, ease, technology, method, exclusivity, audience fit |
| **Value Boosters** | *"What else do I get?"* | Bonus features, included extras, perks |
| **Social Proof & Authority** | *"Can I trust you?"* | Customer counts, ratings, achievements, credentials |
| **Risk Removal** | *"What if I don't like it?"* | Guarantees, trials, easy cancellation |

### 2.1 Extract each angle

For each of the 6 angles, extract from your offer documentation:

### Problem/Pain Points

| Element | Your extraction |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary frustration** | [What keeps them up at night?] |
| **Daily symptoms** | [What annoys them regularly?] |
| **Failed alternatives** | [What have they tried that didn't work?] |
| **Cost of inaction** | [What happens if they don't solve this?] |
| **Headline-ready phrases** | [2-3 short phrases] |

### Value Proposition

| Element | Your extraction |
| --- | --- |
| **Core offer in one sentence** | [What do you provide?] |
| **Primary benefit** | [What's the main outcome?] |
| **How it works (simple)** | [The mechanism in 1-2 sentences] |
| **Headline-ready phrases** | [2-3 short phrases] |

### USPs (Unique Selling Points)

| Element | Your extraction |
| --- | --- |
| **Speed advantage** | [Faster than alternatives?] |
| **Ease advantage** | [Simpler than alternatives?] |
| **Technology/Method** | [Unique approach?] |
| **Specialization** | [Specific audience focus?] |
| **Quantified proof** | [Numbers that prove it?] |
| **Headline-ready phrases** | [2-3 short phrases] |

### Value Boosters

| Element | Your extraction |
| --- | --- |
| **Included extras** | [What's bundled in?] |
| **Bonus features** | [What else do they get?] |
| **Convenience perks** | [What makes it easier?] |
| **Quantities/Specifics** | [Exact numbers?] |
| **Headline-ready phrases** | [2-3 short phrases] |

### Social Proof & Authority

| Element | Your extraction |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer count** | [How many users/customers?] |
| **Ratings/Reviews** | [Score and review count?] |
| **Named customers** | [Recognizable logos/names?] |
| **Credentials** | [Certifications, awards, partnerships?] |
| **Results achieved** | [Quantified outcomes?] |
| **Headline-ready phrases** | [2-3 short phrases] |

### Risk Removal

| Element | Your extraction |
| --- | --- |
| **Guarantee type** | [Money-back? Satisfaction?] |
| **Trial structure** | [Free trial? Duration?] |
| **Commitment level** | [Contract terms? Cancel policy?] |
| **Specific terms** | [Exact conditions?] |
| **Headline-ready phrases** | [2-3 short phrases] |

## Phase 3️⃣: Prioritize for your traffic

### 3.1 Apply your classification

Using your Phase 0 priorities, mark each angle:

| Angle | Priority for Your Traffic | # of Headline Slots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem/Pain Points | [★★★ / ★★ / ★ / —] | [0-2] |
| Value Proposition | [★★★ / ★★ / ★ / —] | [1-2] |
| USPs | [★★★ / ★★ / ★ / —] | [1-2] |
| Value Boosters | [★★★ / ★★ / ★ / —] | [0-1] |
| Social Proof | [★★★ / ★★ / ★ / —] | [1-2] |
| Risk Removal | [★★★ / ★★ / ★ / —] | [1-2] |

> ⚠️ **Total headline slots should equal 7-8:** This keeps you testing-ready and avoids data poverty (See [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md)).

### 3.2 Final output: Your prioritized angle document

By the end of this SOP, you should have:

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Traffic Classification** | Campaign, awareness stage, temperature |
| **Angle Priorities** | Lead / Include / Light / Skip for each angle |
| **6 Extracted Angles** | Each with proof points and headline-ready phrases |
| **Slot Distribution** | # of headline slots per angle |

**Example output structure:**

```
CAMPAIGN: Non-brand Search — "CRM software"
AWARENESS STAGE: Solution Aware
TEMPERATURE: 🌤️ Warm

ANGLE PRIORITIES:
★★★ Lead: Value Proposition, USPs
★★ Include: Social Proof, Risk Removal
★ Light: Problem/Pain
— Skip: Value Boosters

SLOT DISTRIBUTION:
H1: Relevance Anchor (keyword)
H2: Value Proposition
H3: USP
H4: Social Proof
H5: Risk Removal
H6: USP (secondary)
H7: Call-to-Action
H8: Problem/Pain (optional)
Extraction examples by vertical
```

## FAQ

**Q: What if I can't fill an angle category?**

A: That's a signal, but not a failure per se. Either you don't understand your customer well enough (talk to them, read reviews), or your offer has a gap. See the gap identification table in [**Offer Angle Quality Checklist**]<!-- TODO: link target missing --> for diagnosis and next steps.

**Q: How many headline-ready phrases do I need per angle?**

A: Aim for 2-3 per angle. This gives you enough raw material for RSA composition and testing variants in the Iteration Loop (See [SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop](../sops/SOP – RSA Testing with The Iteration Loop.md)).

**Q: Should I run this SOP for every ad group?**

A: Run once per offer. If multiple ad groups share the same offer, the angles are the same, only the traffic temperature classification (Phase 0) may differ per campaign/ad group.

**Q: What if my traffic is mixed (cold and warm)?**

A: Either segment into separate campaigns/ad groups with different RSAs, or optimize for the dominant traffic type. One RSA can't effectively serve all awareness stages simultaneously.

**Q: Can I skip Phase 0 if I already know my audience?**

A: No. Phase 0 isn't about knowing your audience,  it's about classifying this specific traffic source so you prioritize the right angles. The same audience at different awareness stages needs different messaging emphasis.

**Q: What if my offer is the same across multiple campaigns?**

A: Phases 1-2 (Document + Extract) only need to run once per offer. Phase 0 (Classify) and Phase 3 (Prioritize) run per campaign/traffic source, since different traffic temperatures require different angle priorities.

**Q: How do I handle B2B vs. B2C differences?**

A: The 6 angles apply to both. The *proof points* differ. B2B emphasizes ROI, integration, security, and named client logos. B2C emphasizes speed, ease, social proof volume, and emotional outcomes.
