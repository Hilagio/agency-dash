## Purpose

Validates that image creative meets technical specifications and quality standards before upload to Google Ads.

---

## What this checklist validates / does NOT validate

**This checklist validates:**

- Technical specifications (dimensions, file format, resolution)
- Composition and visual quality
- Logo requirements
- Policy compliance
- Common mistake avoidance

**This checklist does NOT:**

- Teach image creative principles (See: [Image Creative Reference](../references/Image Creative Reference.md))
- Configure asset optimization settings (See: [Asset Optimization Control Guidelines](../guidelines/Asset Optimization Control Guidelines.md))
- Validate video assets (See: [Video Creative Quality Checklist](../checklists/Video Creative Quality Checklist.md))

---

## When to use

Run this checklist:

- Before uploading image assets to Google Ads
- When reviewing creative from agency/design team
- When auditing underperforming display/Demand Gen/PMax campaigns
- Before approving final image deliverables

## Checklist

### Technical specifications

- [ ]  Landscape (1.91:1) image created at 1200 × 628 px minimum
- [ ]  Square (1:1) image created at 1200 × 1200 px minimum
- [ ]  Portrait (4:5) image created at 960 × 1200 px (if applicable)
- [ ]  Vertical (9:16) image created at 1080 × 1920 px (if applicable)
- [ ]  File format is JPG or PNG
- [ ]  File size under 5 MB per image
- [ ]  Resolution sharp and not pixelated at intended display sizes

### Logo requirements

- [ ]  Square logo (1:1) provided at 1200 × 1200 px (minimum 128 × 128)
- [ ]  Landscape logo (4:1) provided at 1200 × 300 px (minimum 512 × 128)
- [ ]  Logo legible at small sizes (test at 50 × 50 px)
- [ ]  Logo has transparent or clean solid background
- [ ]  Logo includes appropriate padding/clear space

### Composition quality

- [ ]  Single clear focal point (not competing elements)
- [ ]  Key content within center 80% safe zone
- [ ]  No critical elements at edges (may be cropped)
- [ ]  High contrast between subject and background
- [ ]  Clean, uncluttered background
- [ ]  Faces visible and properly lit (if people included)

### Visual quality

- [ ]  Image is sharp and in focus
- [ ]  Proper lighting (not underexposed or overexposed)
- [ ]  Accurate colors (not oversaturated or washed out)
- [ ]  Minimal compression artifacts
- [ ]  Professional quality (not amateur or low-effort)

### Text on images (if used)

- [ ]  Text covers less than 20% of image area
- [ ]  Text is large and readable at small sizes
- [ ]  High contrast between text and background
- [ ]  Text does not duplicate headline/description copy
- [ ]  No fake buttons, notifications, or misleading elements

### Brand consistency

- [ ]  Images align with brand visual identity
- [ ]  Consistent style across all image variations
- [ ]  Brand colors used appropriately
- [ ]  Photography style matches brand guidelines

### Policy compliance

- [ ]  No simulated buttons or fake UI elements
- [ ]  No misleading before/after imagery
- [ ]  No prohibited content (violence, adult, etc.)
- [ ]  No unrealistic claims visualized
- [ ]  Image accurately represents product/service

### Asset coverage

- [ ]  Minimum 3 images provided (more for PMax/Demand Gen)
- [ ]  All required aspect ratios covered
- [ ]  Variety in imagery (not all identical)
- [ ]  Different angles/contexts represented

### Common mistakes avoided

- [ ]  Not relying on auto-generated images (created all sizes manually)
- [ ]  Not using only one aspect ratio
- [ ]  Not using generic/obvious stock photography
- [ ]  Not placing critical content at edges
- [ ]  Not using low-resolution images

## Dimension quick reference

| Aspect Ratio | Recommended | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landscape (1.91:1) | 1200 × 628 | 600 × 314 |
| Square (1:1) | 1200 × 1200 | 300 × 300 |
| Portrait (4:5) | 960 × 1200 | 480 × 600 |
| Vertical (9:16) | 1080 × 1920 | 540 × 960 |
| Square logo (1:1) | 1200 × 1200 | 128 × 128 |
| Landscape logo (4:1) | 1200 × 300 | 512 × 128 |

## Scoring guidance

| Pass Rate | Rating | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All items checked | Ready to launch | Proceed with upload |
| 1-3 items unchecked | Minor revisions needed | Address gaps before launch |
| 4+ items unchecked | Significant revision needed | Return to design/production |
