## Purpose

Validates that RSA headlines meet constraints, avoid repetition, and cover required persuasion angles.

---

## What this checklist validates

This checklist confirms:

- Constraint compliance (character limits, space utilization)
- Standalone validity (each headline works independently)
- Non-duplication (no repetition across assets)
- Quality (specificity, credibility, searcher focus)
- Angle coverage (persuasion completeness)

This checklist does **NOT**:

- Teach headline writing patterns (See: [Headline Angle Catalog](../catalogs/Headline Angle Catalog.md) )
- Prescribe slot assignments (See: [SOP – Write Compelling RSAs](../sops/SOP – Write Compelling RSAs.md))
- Validate strategic alignment (See: [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md))

## Checklist

### Constraints

- [ ]  Each headline ≤ 30 characters
- [ ]  Most headlines 25-30 characters (space utilized)

### Standalone validity

- [ ]  Each headline makes sense on its own
- [ ]  No headline requires another headline to be understood
- [ ]  DKI defaults are strong standalone phrases (not generic)
- [ ]  No awkward fragments or broken phrases

### Non-duplication

- [ ]  No two headlines express the same claim in different words
- [ ]  No headline duplicates sitelink text verbatim
- [ ]  No headline duplicates callout text verbatim

### Quality

- [ ]  No generic filler ("best", "quality", "leading") without specifics
- [ ]  Quantified claims are believable and defensible
- [ ]  Searcher-focused, not self-focused ("We are...")
- [ ]  No vague claims that competitors could equally make

### Angle coverage

- [ ]  Relevance Anchor exists (DKI or static)
- [ ]  Problem/Pain headline exists (required for cold/warm traffic, optional for hot/branded traffic)
- [ ]  Value Proposition headline exists
- [ ]  CTA headline exists
- [ ]  Trust (Social Proof/Authority) or Risk Removal headline exists

### Modifiers (if used)

- [ ]  Urgency language appears in ≤2 headlines
- [ ]  Price headlines only used where conversion intent supports
