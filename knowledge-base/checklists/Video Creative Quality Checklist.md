## Purpose

Validates that video creative meets Google’s ABCD framework principles and technical requirements before launch.

---

## What this checklist validates / does NOT validate

**This checklist validates:**

- ABCD framework compliance (Attention, Branding, Connection, Direction)
- Technical specifications (aspect ratios, durations, safe zones)
- Production quality standards
- Common mistake avoidance

**This checklist does NOT:**

- Teach video creative principles (See: [Video Creative Reference](../references/Video Creative Reference.md))
- Configure asset optimization settings (See: [Asset Optimization Control Guidelines](../guidelines/Asset Optimization Control Guidelines.md))
- Validate campaign structure (See: relevant campaign SOP)

---

## When to use

Run this checklist:

- Before uploading video assets to Google Ads
- When reviewing video creative from agency/production team
- When auditing underperforming video campaigns
- Before approving final video edits

---

## Checklist

### A — Attention

- [ ]  Impact established in first 5 seconds
- [ ]  Clear hook that demands attention (action, statement, surprise, or problem)
- [ ]  Engaging pacing maintained throughout
- [ ]  Visuals bright and high-contrast (optimized for mobile)
- [ ]  Audio supports and reinforces visual message
- [ ]  Text overlays readable and well-timed (if used)
- [ ]  No slow intro or excessive setup before main message

### B — Branding

- [ ]  Brand/product visible in first 5 seconds
- [ ]  Brand presence maintained throughout (not just end card)
- [ ]  Brand name mentioned in audio ("see and say")
- [ ]  Multiple branding elements used (logo, colors, product, tagline, etc.)
- [ ]  Viewer who skips at 5 seconds would still know who the ad is from

### C — Connection

- [ ]  Features real people (not just product shots)
- [ ]  One clear, focused message (not multiple competing messages)
- [ ]  Target audience can see themselves in the ad
- [ ]  Evokes specific emotion (humor, surprise, empathy, aspiration, etc.)
- [ ]  Simple, accessible language
- [ ]  Shows human benefit, not just product features

### D — Direction

- [ ]  Clear CTA present
- [ ]  CTA specifies exactly what action to take
- [ ]  CTA reinforced with voice-over (audio and visual)
- [ ]  Asset mix includes horizontal video (vertical-only mixes can stop serving in YouTube in-feed)
- [ ]  CTA visible long enough to read and comprehend
- [ ]  Only ONE primary action requested (not multiple CTAs)
- [ ]  CTA appropriate for marketing objective (soft for awareness, strong for action)

### Technical specifications

- [ ]  Horizontal (16:9) version created at 1920×1080 minimum
- [ ]  Vertical (9:16) version created at 1080×1920 minimum
- [ ]  Square (1:1) version created at 1080×1080 minimum (if applicable)
- [ ]  Duration appropriate for intended format (see duration table below)
- [ ]  Critical content within center 70% safe zone (vertical video)
- [ ]  No critical content in top 10% or bottom 20% (vertical video)

### Production quality

- [ ]  Audio clear and properly mixed
- [ ]  No competing audio elements (music vs. voice-over conflict)
- [ ]  Text large enough to read on mobile
- [ ]  High contrast between text and background
- [ ]  Video resolution appropriate (1080p minimum recommended)
- [ ]  No quality degradation from compression

### Common mistakes avoided

- [ ]  No slow intro (hook is immediate)
- [ ]  Branding not saved for end card only
- [ ]  Not feature-focused (benefit-focused instead)
- [ ]  Not relying on auto-generated video (created all formats manually)
- [ ]  Not horizontal-only (vertical/square versions created)

## Duration reference

| Format | Minimum | Recommended | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skippable In-Stream | 12s | 15-60s | No limit (<3 min) |
| Non-Skippable In-Stream | 15s | 15-30s | 60s |
| Bumpers | — | 6s | 6s |
| YouTube Shorts | — | 15-30s | 60s |
| In-Feed | — | 30-90s | No limit |

| Pass Rate | Rating | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 1-3 items unchecked | Minor revisions needed | Address gaps before launch |
| 4+ items unchecked | Significant revision needed | Return to production/editing |
