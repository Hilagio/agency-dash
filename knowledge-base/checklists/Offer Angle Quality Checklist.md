## Purpose

Validates that extracted offer angles are specific, provable, and ready for headline/description composition.

---

## What this checklist validates

This checklist confirms:

- Each angle has sufficient raw material for asset creation
- Proof points are specific and credible
- Gaps are identified before proceeding to composition

This checklist does **NOT**:

- Teach angle extraction (See: [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md))
- Validate headline/description quality (See: [Headline Quality Checklist](../checklists/Headline Quality Checklist.md), [Description Quality Checklist](../checklists/Description Quality Checklist.md))
- Fix weak offers (See: [SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer](../sops/SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer.md))

## When to use

Run this checklist:

- After completing Phase 2 of [SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles](../sops/SOP – Craft Your Offer Angles.md)
- Before proceeding to [SOP – Write Compelling RSAs](../sops/SOP – Write Compelling RSAs.md)
- When auditing existing angle documents for completeness

## Checklist

### Problem/Pain Points

- [ ]  Specific frustrations identified (not generic)
- [ ]  Language matches how customers describe the problem
- [ ]  Failed alternatives documented
- [ ]  Cost of inaction articulated
- [ ]  2-3 headline-ready phrases extracted

### Value Proposition

- [ ]  Main benefit is clear (not just features)
- [ ]  Understandable in 5 seconds
- [ ]  Specific to target audience
- [ ]  2-3 headline-ready phrases extracted

### USPs (Unique Selling Points)

- [ ]  Actually unique (competitors cannot claim it)
- [ ]  Provable (not just asserted)
- [ ]  Matters to target customer
- [ ]  2-3 headline-ready phrases extracted

### Value Boosters

- [ ]  Everything included is listed
- [ ]  Quantities/specifics documented
- [ ]  Would surprise customer positively
- [ ]  2-3 headline-ready phrases extracted

### Social Proof & Authority

- [ ]  Numbers documented (not "trusted by many")
- [ ]  Ratings and review counts included
- [ ]  Recognizable names/logos documented (if B2B)
- [ ]  Credentials that matter documented
- [ ]  2-3 headline-ready phrases extracted

### Risk Removal

- [ ]  Guarantee/trial is specific (terms, duration)
- [ ]  Addresses main hesitation
- [ ]  Stronger than competitors (or at parity)
- [ ]  2-3 headline-ready phrases extracted

---

## Gap identification

If any angle fails its checks, document the gap:

| Missing angle | What it means | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No clear pain points | Don't understand customer | Talk to customers, read reviews |
| No clear USP | You're commoditized | Return to [SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer](../sops/SOP – Create an Irresistible Offer.md) |
| No value boosters | Offer is thin | Consider what you could add/bundle |
| No social proof | Haven't collected it | Start collecting reviews immediately |
| No authority | Haven't built credentials | Pursue certifications, awards |
| No risk removal | Asking too much commitment | Add guarantee, trial, or easier first step |

---

## Definition of done

You may proceed to [SOP – Write Compelling RSAs](../sops/SOP – Write Compelling RSAs.md) when:

- [ ]  All 6 angles documented with proof points
- [ ]  Each angle has 2-3 headline-ready phrases
- [ ]  Gaps identified and either fixed or flagged
- [ ]  Slot distribution mapped (7-8 total headlines)
